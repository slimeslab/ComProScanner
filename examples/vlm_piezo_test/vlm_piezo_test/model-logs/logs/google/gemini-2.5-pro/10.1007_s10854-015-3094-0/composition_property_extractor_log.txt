# TITLE
Investigation of structural and electrical properties of B-site complex ion (Nd1/2Ta1/2)4+-doped Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramic

# ABSTRACT
A reduction of small amount of oxygen vacancies, a significant effect in grain size due to the (NdTa)E^{4+} doping, led to the enhancements of the dielectric, ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties of BiNaTi(NdTa)O ceramics, and the optimized modification was  = 0.006. The electrical properties of modified samples were deteriorated with further doping ( >0.006), owing to an increase in the concentration of oxygen vacancies. The BNT-NT ceramics with  = 0.006 was found to have a high remnant polarization () of 34.7 μC/cmE^{2} and a piezoelectric constant () of 110 pC/N.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-containing piezoelectric ceramics with a perovskite structure, such as Pb(Ti,Zr)O3  and Pb(Zn1/3Nb2/3)O3\u2212PbTiO3 , have been widely utilized in electronic and microelectronic devices, owing to their superior dielectric, piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties [\u2013]. Therefore, extensive research has been directed on lead-free piezoelectric materials with sufficient properties to replace lead-containing ceramics [\u2013]. Among the various lead-free environmentally friendly piezoelectric ceramic systems, Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3  (abbreviated as BNT) ceramics have attracted great attention and have been considered to be one of the highly promising candidates as a replacement for lead-containing piezoelectric ceramics. Pioneering works done by Takenaka and Sakata [, ] have demonstrated that BNT is an attractive piezoelectric material because of its relatively large remnant polarization (Pr) and high Curie temperature (Tc). To overcome these problems and improve the piezoelectric properties, attempts have been made to modify BNT via complex ion substitute B-site such as (Yb1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ], (Lu1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ], however, which indicate B-site substitution of complex ions has a significant impact on the electrical properties. Up to now, B-site complex ion substitution of (A1/2B1/2)E^{4+} (A = trivalent metal, B = quinquevalent metal) for TiE^{4+} at B-sites in BNT perovskite has received attention. In addition, lead-based ceramics with B-site substitution of complex ions of (Yb1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ] and (Lu1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ] have high dielectric constant, high piezoelectric coefficient, and strong ferroelectric properties. Yang et al.  found that at a low Nd2O3 concentration, the Nd doped 0.82 BNT\u20130.18 BKT ceramics show a high piezoelectric constant. Nd, Yb and Lu, as a kind of rare earth element, are all belong to lanthanide series in the periodic table and show a same valence electron configuration of 4fE^{0}6sE^{2} with the similar chemical characteristic. However, to our best knowledge, the effect of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} on the structure and electrical properties of BNT-based ceramics was rarely studied so far. Therefore, in this study, a suitable amount of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} substituted for TiE^{4+} was used to improve the properties of BNT ceramics. The effects of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} doping on phase structure, microstructure and electrical properties were systematically investigated.

# CONCLUSION
In summary, the effect of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} doping on the Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 ceramics was systematically studied. The structures of the prepared ceramics were characterized by using SEM, XRD and Broadband Dielectric Spectrometer. As compared to pure BNT ceramics, an increase in the remnant polarization Pr and dielectric constant \u03b5r a decrease in coercive field Ec were observed in BNT-x NT ceramics with x = 0.006. As the addition is below 0.006, the introduced (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} complex ion resulted in a reduction of small amount of oxygen vacancies, which should be responsible for the increased dielectric constant \u03b5r, piezoelectric constant d33 and the remnant polarization Pr. The electrical properties of Bi1/2Na1/2Ti1\u2212x(Nd1/2Ta1/2)xO3 ceramics was optative at the (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} addition of x = 0.006 (Pr = 34.7 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}, d33 = 110 pC/N).

# TITLE
Investigation of structural and electrical properties of B-site complex ion (Nd1/2Ta1/2)4+-doped Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramic

# ABSTRACT
A reduction of small amount of oxygen vacancies, a significant effect in grain size due to the (NdTa)E^{4+} doping, led to the enhancements of the dielectric, ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties of BiNaTi(NdTa)O ceramics, and the optimized modification was  = 0.006. The electrical properties of modified samples were deteriorated with further doping ( >0.006), owing to an increase in the concentration of oxygen vacancies. The BNT-NT ceramics with  = 0.006 was found to have a high remnant polarization () of 34.7 μC/cmE^{2} and a piezoelectric constant () of 110 pC/N.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-containing piezoelectric ceramics with a perovskite structure, such as Pb(Ti,Zr)O3  and Pb(Zn1/3Nb2/3)O3\u2212PbTiO3 , have been widely utilized in electronic and microelectronic devices, owing to their superior dielectric, piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties [\u2013]. Therefore, extensive research has been directed on lead-free piezoelectric materials with sufficient properties to replace lead-containing ceramics [\u2013]. Among the various lead-free environmentally friendly piezoelectric ceramic systems, Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3  (abbreviated as BNT) ceramics have attracted great attention and have been considered to be one of the highly promising candidates as a replacement for lead-containing piezoelectric ceramics. Pioneering works done by Takenaka and Sakata [, ] have demonstrated that BNT is an attractive piezoelectric material because of its relatively large remnant polarization (Pr) and high Curie temperature (Tc). To overcome these problems and improve the piezoelectric properties, attempts have been made to modify BNT via complex ion substitute B-site such as (Yb1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ], (Lu1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ], however, which indicate B-site substitution of complex ions has a significant impact on the electrical properties. Up to now, B-site complex ion substitution of (A1/2B1/2)E^{4+} (A = trivalent metal, B = quinquevalent metal) for TiE^{4+} at B-sites in BNT perovskite has received attention. In addition, lead-based ceramics with B-site substitution of complex ions of (Yb1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ] and (Lu1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} [, ] have high dielectric constant, high piezoelectric coefficient, and strong ferroelectric properties. Yang et al.  found that at a low Nd2O3 concentration, the Nd doped 0.82 BNT\u20130.18 BKT ceramics show a high piezoelectric constant. Nd, Yb and Lu, as a kind of rare earth element, are all belong to lanthanide series in the periodic table and show a same valence electron configuration of 4fE^{0}6sE^{2} with the similar chemical characteristic. However, to our best knowledge, the effect of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} on the structure and electrical properties of BNT-based ceramics was rarely studied so far. Therefore, in this study, a suitable amount of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} substituted for TiE^{4+} was used to improve the properties of BNT ceramics. The effects of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} doping on phase structure, microstructure and electrical properties were systematically investigated.

# CONCLUSION
In summary, the effect of (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} doping on the Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 ceramics was systematically studied. The structures of the prepared ceramics were characterized by using SEM, XRD and Broadband Dielectric Spectrometer. As compared to pure BNT ceramics, an increase in the remnant polarization Pr and dielectric constant \u03b5r a decrease in coercive field Ec were observed in BNT-x NT ceramics with x = 0.006. As the addition is below 0.006, the introduced (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} complex ion resulted in a reduction of small amount of oxygen vacancies, which should be responsible for the increased dielectric constant \u03b5r, piezoelectric constant d33 and the remnant polarization Pr. The electrical properties of Bi1/2Na1/2Ti1\u2212x(Nd1/2Ta1/2)xO3 ceramics was optative at the (Nd1/2Ta1/2)E^{4+} addition of x = 0.006 (Pr = 34.7 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}, d33 = 110 pC/N).
