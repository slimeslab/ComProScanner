# TITLE
Large piezoelectric performance in zirconium doped Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 lead-free ceramics through utilizing multiphase coexistence

# ABSTRACT
Lead-free piezoceramics of BaSrTiZrO (BSTZ) are prepared by the conventional solid-state method. The microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of BSTZ ceramics in the composition range of 0 ≤ ≤ 0.1 are systematically studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral phase, tetragonal phase, and orthorhombic phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 ≤ ≤ 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high  value (~ 4743), which is superior or comparable to the other BT-based ceramics. As a result, the addition of the Zr can effectively improve the electricity of the BaTiO-based ceramics, which can be beneficial to facilitate the practical process of BT-based ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
Since the 1950s, Pb(Zr1\u2212xTix)O3 (PZT) piezoelectric ceramics have dominated the field of piezoelectric ceramic materials, due to excellent piezoelectric properties and high Curie temperature. However, the content of lead is 60% in PZT-based piezoelectric ceramics, which is a highly toxic substance that can cause serious environmental and human health problems. In 1956, Bechmann et al. obtained BaTiO3 (BT) materials with its excellent electric properties, which is considered to be one of the most promising lead-free piezoelectric ceramic systems. Subsequently, the researchers conducted much work on BT-based ceramics, including reducing sintering temperature, chemical modification and so on. Among those, the doping project has achieved a series of breakthroughs in d33 through phase boundary regulation. It is realized that the increase of piezoelectric performance can be attributed to the construction of multiphase boundaries by chemical modification (e.g., Ca, Zr, Sn and Hf) [, , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , \u2013]. Liu et al. reported a barium titanate-based material with a d33 value of around 620 pC/N by constructing the cubic\u2013rhombohedral\u2013tetragonal (C\u2013R\u2013T) phase boundary, even higher than some lead-based ceramics. It is reported that a little Sr doping can improve the dielectric and ferroelectric properties of BT materials, but their piezoelectric property is still inferior to the PZT-based ceramics [, ]. Therefore, Sr doped BT-based ceramics with high piezoelectric property have been an important research direction in lead-free ceramics. In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid state method. The effect of Zr on the phase structure, dielectric, ferroelectric, and piezoelectric properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 is comprehensively studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral, orthorhombic, and tetragonal phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with x = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high \u03b5r value (~ 4743), which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid-state method. The evolution of microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 ceramics in the range of 0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1 are systematically investigated. Zr can move the TR\u2013O and TO\u2013T to room temperature, eventually forming multiphase boundary of R\u2013O\u2013T, resulting in an enhanced electrical performance (e.g., d33 ~ 620 pC/N, \u03b5r ~ 4743, tan\u03b4 ~ 0.016, strain ~ 0.152%, and d33* ~ 407 pm/V) at x = 0.08. Therefore, the addition of Zr content can effectively improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of BT-based ceramics, which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# TITLE
Large piezoelectric performance in zirconium doped Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 lead-free ceramics through utilizing multiphase coexistence

# ABSTRACT
Lead-free piezoceramics of BaSrTiZrO (BSTZ) are prepared by the conventional solid-state method. The microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of BSTZ ceramics in the composition range of 0 ≤ ≤ 0.1 are systematically studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral phase, tetragonal phase, and orthorhombic phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 ≤ ≤ 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high  value (~ 4743), which is superior or comparable to the other BT-based ceramics. As a result, the addition of the Zr can effectively improve the electricity of the BaTiO-based ceramics, which can be beneficial to facilitate the practical process of BT-based ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
Since the 1950s, Pb(Zr1\u2212xTix)O3 (PZT) piezoelectric ceramics have dominated the field of piezoelectric ceramic materials, due to excellent piezoelectric properties and high Curie temperature. However, the content of lead is 60% in PZT-based piezoelectric ceramics, which is a highly toxic substance that can cause serious environmental and human health problems. In 1956, Bechmann et al. obtained BaTiO3 (BT) materials with its excellent electric properties, which is considered to be one of the most promising lead-free piezoelectric ceramic systems. Subsequently, the researchers conducted much work on BT-based ceramics, including reducing sintering temperature, chemical modification and so on. Among those, the doping project has achieved a series of breakthroughs in d33 through phase boundary regulation. It is realized that the increase of piezoelectric performance can be attributed to the construction of multiphase boundaries by chemical modification (e.g., Ca, Zr, Sn and Hf) [, , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , \u2013]. Liu et al. reported a barium titanate-based material with a d33 value of around 620 pC/N by constructing the cubic\u2013rhombohedral\u2013tetragonal (C\u2013R\u2013T) phase boundary, even higher than some lead-based ceramics. It is reported that a little Sr doping can improve the dielectric and ferroelectric properties of BT materials, but their piezoelectric property is still inferior to the PZT-based ceramics [, ]. Therefore, Sr doped BT-based ceramics with high piezoelectric property have been an important research direction in lead-free ceramics. In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid state method. The effect of Zr on the phase structure, dielectric, ferroelectric, and piezoelectric properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 is comprehensively studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral, orthorhombic, and tetragonal phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with x = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high \u03b5r value (~ 4743), which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid-state method. The evolution of microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 ceramics in the range of 0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1 are systematically investigated. Zr can move the TR\u2013O and TO\u2013T to room temperature, eventually forming multiphase boundary of R\u2013O\u2013T, resulting in an enhanced electrical performance (e.g., d33 ~ 620 pC/N, \u03b5r ~ 4743, tan\u03b4 ~ 0.016, strain ~ 0.152%, and d33* ~ 407 pm/V) at x = 0.08. Therefore, the addition of Zr content can effectively improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of BT-based ceramics, which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# TITLE
Large piezoelectric performance in zirconium doped Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 lead-free ceramics through utilizing multiphase coexistence

# ABSTRACT
Lead-free piezoceramics of BaSrTiZrO (BSTZ) are prepared by the conventional solid-state method. The microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of BSTZ ceramics in the composition range of 0 ≤ ≤ 0.1 are systematically studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral phase, tetragonal phase, and orthorhombic phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 ≤ ≤ 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high  value (~ 4743), which is superior or comparable to the other BT-based ceramics. As a result, the addition of the Zr can effectively improve the electricity of the BaTiO-based ceramics, which can be beneficial to facilitate the practical process of BT-based ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
Since the 1950s, Pb(Zr1\u2212xTix)O3 (PZT) piezoelectric ceramics have dominated the field of piezoelectric ceramic materials, due to excellent piezoelectric properties and high Curie temperature. However, the content of lead is 60% in PZT-based piezoelectric ceramics, which is a highly toxic substance that can cause serious environmental and human health problems. In 1956, Bechmann et al. obtained BaTiO3 (BT) materials with its excellent electric properties, which is considered to be one of the most promising lead-free piezoelectric ceramic systems. Subsequently, the researchers conducted much work on BT-based ceramics, including reducing sintering temperature, chemical modification and so on. Among those, the doping project has achieved a series of breakthroughs in d33 through phase boundary regulation. It is realized that the increase of piezoelectric performance can be attributed to the construction of multiphase boundaries by chemical modification (e.g., Ca, Zr, Sn and Hf) [, , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , \u2013]. Liu et al. reported a barium titanate-based material with a d33 value of around 620 pC/N by constructing the cubic\u2013rhombohedral\u2013tetragonal (C\u2013R\u2013T) phase boundary, even higher than some lead-based ceramics. It is reported that a little Sr doping can improve the dielectric and ferroelectric properties of BT materials, but their piezoelectric property is still inferior to the PZT-based ceramics [, ]. Therefore, Sr doped BT-based ceramics with high piezoelectric property have been an important research direction in lead-free ceramics. In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid state method. The effect of Zr on the phase structure, dielectric, ferroelectric, and piezoelectric properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 is comprehensively studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral, orthorhombic, and tetragonal phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with x = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high \u03b5r value (~ 4743), which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid-state method. The evolution of microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 ceramics in the range of 0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1 are systematically investigated. Zr can move the TR\u2013O and TO\u2013T to room temperature, eventually forming multiphase boundary of R\u2013O\u2013T, resulting in an enhanced electrical performance (e.g., d33 ~ 620 pC/N, \u03b5r ~ 4743, tan\u03b4 ~ 0.016, strain ~ 0.152%, and d33* ~ 407 pm/V) at x = 0.08. Therefore, the addition of Zr content can effectively improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of BT-based ceramics, which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

It is realized that the increase of piezoelectric performance can be attributed to the construction of multiphase boundaries by chemical modification (e.g., Ca, Zr, Sn and Hf) [, , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , , \u2013]. Liu et al. reported a barium titanate-based material with a d33 value of around 620 pC/N by constructing the cubic\u2013rhombohedral\u2013tetragonal (C\u2013R\u2013T) phase boundary, even higher than some lead-based ceramics. It is reported that a little Sr doping can improve the dielectric and ferroelectric properties of BT materials, but their piezoelectric property is still inferior to the PZT-based ceramics [, ]. Therefore, Sr doped BT-based ceramics with high piezoelectric property have been an important research direction in lead-free ceramics. In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid state method. The effect of Zr on the phase structure, dielectric, ferroelectric, and piezoelectric properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14TiO3 is comprehensively studied. The coexistence of the rhombohedral, orthorhombic, and tetragonal phase is determined in the ceramic with 0.06 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, showing enhanced electrical properties in the ceramics with x = 0.08, especially the large piezoelectric property (~ 620 pC/N) and high \u03b5r value (~ 4743), which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
In this work, Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 (BSTZx) lead-free piezoceramics are synthesized by conventional solid-state method. The evolution of microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of Ba0.86Sr0.14Ti1\u2212xZrxO3 ceramics in the range of 0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1 are systematically investigated. Zr can move the TR\u2013O and TO\u2013T to room temperature, eventually forming multiphase boundary of R\u2013O\u2013T, resulting in an enhanced electrical performance (e.g., d33 ~ 620 pC/N, \u03b5r ~ 4743, tan\u03b4 ~ 0.016, strain ~ 0.152%, and d33* ~ 407 pm/V) at x = 0.08. Therefore, the addition of Zr content can effectively improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of BT-based ceramics, which is a potential candidate for lead-free piezoceramics.
