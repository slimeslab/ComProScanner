# TITLE
Structure evolution and electrical properties of lead-free Bi0.5Na0.41K0.09TiO3 piezoceramics by isovalent La doping

# ABSTRACT
The effects of LaE^{3+}-isovalent substituted on BiE^{3+}-site of the BiNaKTiO system were examined. The addition of La doping in the BiNaKTiO ceramics induced the second phase of KTiO and a crystal structural change from the rhombohedral and tetragonal phase to a pseudocubic phase. All samples show similar grain morphology and dense microstructure with the average grain size around 1.33 to 1.61 μm. The dielectric curves and field-induced polarization loops of samples confirm the nonergodic-to-ergodic relaxor phase transformation, corresponding to the reduction of the temperature at the phase transition from ferroelectric to relaxor phase and the disruption of the ferroelectric properties happened in samples when increasing LaE^{3+} content. Besides, the maximum piezoelectric constant of 139 pC/N was found in (BiLa)(NaK)TiO ceramic, while the (BiLa)(NaK)TiO ceramic shows a maximum strain of ~ 0.2% at a low field of 40 kV/cm.

# INTRODUCTION
Among them, lead zirconate titanate (PZT) piezoceramics have been one of the most popular materials for the applications of electronic devices due to its large piezoelectric response. Therefore, it is necessary to find new lead-free piezoceramics such as BaTiO3 (BT), Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 (BNT), Bi1/2K1/2TiO3 (BKT), K1/2Na1/2NbO3 (KNN), and their solid solutions to replace the current lead-based piezoceramics materials [, , , , , \u2013]. Among some lead-free piezoelectric ceramics, Bi1/2(Na,K)1/2TiO3 ceramic has been extensively studied due to the advantage of replacing the lead-based PZT materials [, ]. The Bi1/2(Na1\u2212xKx)1/2TiO3 (BNKTx) solid solutions have the morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) at x = 0.16\u20130.2. The improvement in dielectric properties and piezoelectric properties of BNKTx was found to be similar to that observed in PZT. The giant electromechanical strain has appeared when MPB of BNKTx ceramics was modified with other ABO3 compounds [, , , , \u2013] or impurities [, , , , , \u2013]. However, the estimated strain of modified BNKTx required a large driving field (E \u2265 60 kV/cm), which is the disadvantage in the practical application of BNKTx materials. Several studies suggested the ability of electromechanical properties enhancement by A-site added/substituted on BNKTx-based materials [, , ]. To further understand the effects of La doping in the properties of BNKTx materials, this study investigates the crystal structure, microstructure, dielectric properties, ferroelectric properties, and electromechanical strain properties of the (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 solid solution. Unlike our previous study [, ], where the effects of LaE^{3+}-donor and LiE^{+}-acceptor which modified the [Bi,Na,K]E^{2+}-site of the ceramics were investigated, this study will investigate the effects of LaE^{3+}-isovalent which modified the BiE^{3+}-site of the lead-free Bi-based piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
Lead-free (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 (x = 0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.04, and 0.05) piezoceramics were successfully fabricated by a traditional solid-state synthesis. The addition of isovalent La doping in the (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 ceramics induced the second phase of K4Ti3O8 in the crystal structure. All samples show similar grain morphology and dense microstructure with the average grain size around 1.33 to 1.61 \u03bcm. A large piezoelectric constant of 139 pC/N was found in Bi0.48La0.02Na0.41K0.09TiO3 sample while a giant electromechanical strain of 500 pm/V at 40 kV/cm was found in Bi0.46La0.04Na0.41K0.09TiO3 sample.

# TITLE
Structure evolution and electrical properties of lead-free Bi0.5Na0.41K0.09TiO3 piezoceramics by isovalent La doping

# ABSTRACT
The effects of LaE^{3+}-isovalent substituted on BiE^{3+}-site of the BiNaKTiO system were examined. The addition of La doping in the BiNaKTiO ceramics induced the second phase of KTiO and a crystal structural change from the rhombohedral and tetragonal phase to a pseudocubic phase. All samples show similar grain morphology and dense microstructure with the average grain size around 1.33 to 1.61 μm. The dielectric curves and field-induced polarization loops of samples confirm the nonergodic-to-ergodic relaxor phase transformation, corresponding to the reduction of the temperature at the phase transition from ferroelectric to relaxor phase and the disruption of the ferroelectric properties happened in samples when increasing LaE^{3+} content. Besides, the maximum piezoelectric constant of 139 pC/N was found in (BiLa)(NaK)TiO ceramic, while the (BiLa)(NaK)TiO ceramic shows a maximum strain of ~ 0.2% at a low field of 40 kV/cm.

# INTRODUCTION
Among them, lead zirconate titanate (PZT) piezoceramics have been one of the most popular materials for the applications of electronic devices due to its large piezoelectric response. Therefore, it is necessary to find new lead-free piezoceramics such as BaTiO3 (BT), Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 (BNT), Bi1/2K1/2TiO3 (BKT), K1/2Na1/2NbO3 (KNN), and their solid solutions to replace the current lead-based piezoceramics materials [, , , , , \u2013]. Among some lead-free piezoelectric ceramics, Bi1/2(Na,K)1/2TiO3 ceramic has been extensively studied due to the advantage of replacing the lead-based PZT materials [, ]. The Bi1/2(Na1\u2212xKx)1/2TiO3 (BNKTx) solid solutions have the morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) at x = 0.16\u20130.2. The improvement in dielectric properties and piezoelectric properties of BNKTx was found to be similar to that observed in PZT. The giant electromechanical strain has appeared when MPB of BNKTx ceramics was modified with other ABO3 compounds [, , , , \u2013] or impurities [, , , , , \u2013]. However, the estimated strain of modified BNKTx required a large driving field (E \u2265 60 kV/cm), which is the disadvantage in the practical application of BNKTx materials. Several studies suggested the ability of electromechanical properties enhancement by A-site added/substituted on BNKTx-based materials [, , ]. To further understand the effects of La doping in the properties of BNKTx materials, this study investigates the crystal structure, microstructure, dielectric properties, ferroelectric properties, and electromechanical strain properties of the (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 solid solution. Unlike our previous study [, ], where the effects of LaE^{3+}-donor and LiE^{+}-acceptor which modified the [Bi,Na,K]E^{2+}-site of the ceramics were investigated, this study will investigate the effects of LaE^{3+}-isovalent which modified the BiE^{3+}-site of the lead-free Bi-based piezoceramics.

# CONCLUSION
Lead-free (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 (x = 0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.04, and 0.05) piezoceramics were successfully fabricated by a traditional solid-state synthesis. The addition of isovalent La doping in the (Bi0.5\u2212xLax)(Na0.41K0.09)TiO3 ceramics induced the second phase of K4Ti3O8 in the crystal structure. All samples show similar grain morphology and dense microstructure with the average grain size around 1.33 to 1.61 \u03bcm. A large piezoelectric constant of 139 pC/N was found in Bi0.48La0.02Na0.41K0.09TiO3 sample while a giant electromechanical strain of 500 pm/V at 40 kV/cm was found in Bi0.46La0.04Na0.41K0.09TiO3 sample.
