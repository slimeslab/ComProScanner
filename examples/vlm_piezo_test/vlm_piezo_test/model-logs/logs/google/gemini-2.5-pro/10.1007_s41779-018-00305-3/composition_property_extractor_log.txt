# TITLE
Effect of processing conditions on (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 lead-free ceramics for the enhancement of structural, humidity sensing and dielectric properties

# ABSTRACT
The present study reports detailed guidelines for the preparation of high-quality perovskite (BaCa)(TiSn)O (BCTS) ( = 0.0–0.1) lead-free ceramics by solid state reaction. The compositions ( = 0.0–0.04) exhibit orthorhombic–tetragonal phase transition (T), except  ≥ 0.06 that shows a pure tetragonal structure phase which conformed by X-ray diffraction (XRD). The microstructure and purity of the sintered ceramics were examined using scanning electron microscope equipped with an energy dispersive spectrometer (SEM-EDS). Field emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM) was used to examine the morphology of sensing film of the calcined powder and it was prepared as a humidity sensor using screen-printing technique. All the compositions exhibited poor sensitivity toward the humidity sensing in the range of 0–98% RH at room temperature. Hot-stage microscope (HSM) has been used to investigate the sintering curve of the pure calcined powder and it was found that the suitable sintering temperature for obtaining a fully dense microstructure is 1400 °C. The highest values of permittivity ( = 46,515, at 10 kHz) and piezoelectric coefficient ( = 510 pC/N) were achieved in the composition  = 0.02.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead titanate ceramics (PbTiO3) exhibit a large mechanical quality factor (> 1000) , large pyroelectric coefficient (p > 5.7 \xd7 10E^{\u22124} C/mE^{2} K)  and high spontaneous polarization (> 0.53 C/mE^{2})  which make them potentially suitable for various applications, such as resonance-based transducer, infrared sensors and non-volatile memories. Generally, lead-free ceramics such as BaTiO3 (BT) exhibits inferior piezoelectric coefficient (d33 = 191 pC/N)  compared to (Pb, Zr)TiO3 (d33 = 600 pC/N), which causes a limitation for the industrial piezoelectric applications. Recently, BT has been developed by co-doping of CaE^{2+} in the A-site and (ZrE^{4+}/SnE^{4+}) into the B-site of lattice for optimizing the piezoelectric properties. Li et al. prepared (Ba0.97Ca0.03)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 ceramic using a solid-state reaction technique for enhancement dielectric and piezoelectric properties, and they reported that both dielectric constant and piezoelectric coefficient are 6900 and 440pC/N respectively. (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free ceramic has been synthesized using a traditional solid solution method by Bao et al. and high d33 568pC/N was achieved. (Ba0.99Ca0.01)(Ti0.98Zr0.02)O3 (BCZT)  has been taken under consideration for optimizing the piezoelectric properties. High performance of BaTiO3\u2013x(0.4CaTiO3\u20130.6BaSnO3) lead-free ceramics were prepared via solid solution technique, where multiphase coexistence of BT and CT was observed at x = 0.16 and 0.2. Higher polarization (Pr = 9.48 \u03bcm/cmE^{2}) and higher piezoelectrical response (d33 = 570 pC/N) were achieved at the composition of x = 0.16, suggesting that BT-x(CT-BS) system is a promising alternative material for electromechanical actuators. The highest piezoelectric coefficient (d33 = 697pC/N) was achieved in multiphase coexistence of BaTiO3\u201311BaSnO3 composite ceramic. Another system of BaTiO3\u2013KNbO3 (0.5BT-0.5KN) lead-free composite ceramic exhibited approximately three times larger piezoelectric constant (d33 = 136 pC/N)  more than the KNbO3 single phase (d33 = 40 pC/N). It is well known that the humidity can induce conductive paths and consequently, leakage of current through the dielectric materials, thus it is beneficial to study the sensitivity of the powder toward the relative humidity (RH). Wang et al. have studied the variation of resistance with the RH of pure BT sensor  and they have concluded that the material exhibits good sensitivity and linearity toward the humidity sensing in the range of 33\u201398% RH. A Sequential study has been carried out on the sensitivity of pure PbTiO3 (PT) toward the RH and it was concluded that PT is considered a good sensor toward RH only at a high range of humidity (above 80%). A previous research on the enhancement of sensing properties of pure PT for industrial deployment as a good low-range humidity sensor was carried out. The study has concluded that metal oxides such as LiE^{1+} and CaE^{2+} ions are capable of improving the sensing behavior of the PT toward humidity [, ]. In this study, the effects of the processing conditions on (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 (0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1) lead-free ceramics prepared by solid state reaction have been thoroughly investigated and the optimum calcination/sintering windows to obtain high-quality BCTS ceramics have been identified.

# CONCLUSION
The processing conditions to fabricate single-phase, dense, and pure (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 ceramics (0.0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1) by solid state reaction were identified. Single-phase powder was obtained by calcining a stoichiometric mixture at 1200 \xb0C for 4 h. However, single-phase and dense ceramics were obtained by sintering the calcined powder at 1400 \xb0C. Phase transition of BCTS from orthorhombic to tetragonal was identified in the range (0\u20130.04) of Ca addition, but the compositions of x \u2265 0.06 exhibit a pure tetragonal structure. All the BCTS compositions exhibit high dielectric and piezoelectric properties compared to pure BT. The highest values of permittivity and piezoelectricity are obtained at x = 0.02 which is in good agreement with literature. Moreover, all the sensing films of different compostions di not show a good response towards RH.

# TITLE
Effect of processing conditions on (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 lead-free ceramics for the enhancement of structural, humidity sensing and dielectric properties

# ABSTRACT
The present study reports detailed guidelines for the preparation of high-quality perovskite (BaCa)(TiSn)O (BCTS) ( = 0.0–0.1) lead-free ceramics by solid state reaction. The compositions ( = 0.0–0.04) exhibit orthorhombic–tetragonal phase transition (T), except  ≥ 0.06 that shows a pure tetragonal structure phase which conformed by X-ray diffraction (XRD). The microstructure and purity of the sintered ceramics were examined using scanning electron microscope equipped with an energy dispersive spectrometer (SEM-EDS). Field emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM) was used to examine the morphology of sensing film of the calcined powder and it was prepared as a humidity sensor using screen-printing technique. All the compositions exhibited poor sensitivity toward the humidity sensing in the range of 0–98% RH at room temperature. Hot-stage microscope (HSM) has been used to investigate the sintering curve of the pure calcined powder and it was found that the suitable sintering temperature for obtaining a fully dense microstructure is 1400 °C. The highest values of permittivity ( = 46,515, at 10 kHz) and piezoelectric coefficient ( = 510 pC/N) were achieved in the composition  = 0.02.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead titanate ceramics (PbTiO3) exhibit a large mechanical quality factor (> 1000) , large pyroelectric coefficient (p > 5.7 \xd7 10E^{\u22124} C/mE^{2} K)  and high spontaneous polarization (> 0.53 C/mE^{2})  which make them potentially suitable for various applications, such as resonance-based transducer, infrared sensors and non-volatile memories. Generally, lead-free ceramics such as BaTiO3 (BT) exhibits inferior piezoelectric coefficient (d33 = 191 pC/N)  compared to (Pb, Zr)TiO3 (d33 = 600 pC/N), which causes a limitation for the industrial piezoelectric applications. Recently, BT has been developed by co-doping of CaE^{2+} in the A-site and (ZrE^{4+}/SnE^{4+}) into the B-site of lattice for optimizing the piezoelectric properties. Li et al. prepared (Ba0.97Ca0.03)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 ceramic using a solid-state reaction technique for enhancement dielectric and piezoelectric properties, and they reported that both dielectric constant and piezoelectric coefficient are 6900 and 440pC/N respectively. (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free ceramic has been synthesized using a traditional solid solution method by Bao et al. and high d33 568pC/N was achieved. (Ba0.99Ca0.01)(Ti0.98Zr0.02)O3 (BCZT)  has been taken under consideration for optimizing the piezoelectric properties. High performance of BaTiO3\u2013x(0.4CaTiO3\u20130.6BaSnO3) lead-free ceramics were prepared via solid solution technique, where multiphase coexistence of BT and CT was observed at x = 0.16 and 0.2. Higher polarization (Pr = 9.48 \u03bcm/cmE^{2}) and higher piezoelectrical response (d33 = 570 pC/N) were achieved at the composition of x = 0.16, suggesting that BT-x(CT-BS) system is a promising alternative material for electromechanical actuators. The highest piezoelectric coefficient (d33 = 697pC/N) was achieved in multiphase coexistence of BaTiO3\u201311BaSnO3 composite ceramic. Another system of BaTiO3\u2013KNbO3 (0.5BT-0.5KN) lead-free composite ceramic exhibited approximately three times larger piezoelectric constant (d33 = 136 pC/N)  more than the KNbO3 single phase (d33 = 40 pC/N). It is well known that the humidity can induce conductive paths and consequently, leakage of current through the dielectric materials, thus it is beneficial to study the sensitivity of the powder toward the relative humidity (RH). Wang et al. have studied the variation of resistance with the RH of pure BT sensor  and they have concluded that the material exhibits good sensitivity and linearity toward the humidity sensing in the range of 33\u201398% RH. A Sequential study has been carried out on the sensitivity of pure PbTiO3 (PT) toward the RH and it was concluded that PT is considered a good sensor toward RH only at a high range of humidity (above 80%). A previous research on the enhancement of sensing properties of pure PT for industrial deployment as a good low-range humidity sensor was carried out. The study has concluded that metal oxides such as LiE^{1+} and CaE^{2+} ions are capable of improving the sensing behavior of the PT toward humidity [, ]. In this study, the effects of the processing conditions on (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 (0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1) lead-free ceramics prepared by solid state reaction have been thoroughly investigated and the optimum calcination/sintering windows to obtain high-quality BCTS ceramics have been identified.

# CONCLUSION
The processing conditions to fabricate single-phase, dense, and pure (Ba1-xCax)(Ti0.9Sn0.1)O3 ceramics (0.0 \u2264 x \u2264 0.1) by solid state reaction were identified. Single-phase powder was obtained by calcining a stoichiometric mixture at 1200 \xb0C for 4 h. However, single-phase and dense ceramics were obtained by sintering the calcined powder at 1400 \xb0C. Phase transition of BCTS from orthorhombic to tetragonal was identified in the range (0\u20130.04) of Ca addition, but the compositions of x \u2265 0.06 exhibit a pure tetragonal structure. All the BCTS compositions exhibit high dielectric and piezoelectric properties compared to pure BT. The highest values of permittivity and piezoelectricity are obtained at x = 0.02 which is in good agreement with literature. Moreover, all the sensing films of different compostions di not show a good response towards RH.
