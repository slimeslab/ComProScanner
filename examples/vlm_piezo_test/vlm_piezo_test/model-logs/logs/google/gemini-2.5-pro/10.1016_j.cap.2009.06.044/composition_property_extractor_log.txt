# INTRODUCTION
Lead-containing piezoelectric materials with perovskite structure such as lead zirconate titanate (PZT) and PZT-based multi-component systems are widely used for piezoelectric actuators, sensors and transducers in various electronic devices due to their excellent piezoelectric properties. Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 (BNT) has a rhombohedral perovskite structure and is considered to be one of the promising alternatives to lead-free piezoelectric ceramics due to its strong ferroelectricity (P r =38\u03bcC/cmE^{2}) at room temperature. However, its coercive field is also high (E c =7.3kV/mm), making the polarization of the ceramic very difficult. Therefore, the pure BNT ceramic exhibits relatively poor piezoelectric properties (d 33 =58pC/N). A number of studies have been carried out to improve the poling process and enhance the piezoelectric properties of the ceramics; these include the formation of solid solutions of BNT with other ABO3-type ferroelectrics or non-ferroelectrics, e.g. BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , BNT\u2013Ba2NbO6 , BNT\u2013BiAlO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013SrTiO3 , BNT\u2013Ba(Cu1/2W1/2)O3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013BaTiO3 , and BNT\u2013K0.5Na0.5NbO3 , the substitutions of analogous ions for the A-site (Bi0.5Na0.5)E^{+} or B-site TiE^{4+} ions, e.g. (Bi0.5Na0.5)0.94Ba0.06Zr y Ti1\u2212 y O3 and (Bi1/2Na1/2)Ti1\u2212 x (Zn1/3Nb2/3) x O3 , and the doping of metal oxides, e.g. Ta-doped 0.94BNT\u20130.06BaTiO3 and La2O3-doped 0.92BNT\u20130.08BaTiO3. It can be seen that the formation of a new solid solution has been shown to be an effective method to improve the piezoelectric properties of the BNT\u2013based ceramics. It is hence anticipated that, by developing a new multi-component system with low E c and large P r, the piezoelectric properties of the BNT-based ceramics can be further enhanced. In the present work, a new lead-free solid solution, (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3 (abbreviated as BNNBT-x/y), was developed by the co-substitutions of 2\u20134mol% NdE^{3+} for BiE^{3+} and BaE^{2+} (2\u201312%) for (Bi0.5Na0.5)E^{2+} in the A-sites of BNT ceramics and fabricated by an ordinary sintering method, and its dielectric and piezoelectric properties were studied.

# CONCLUSION
A new NdE^{3+}- and BaE^{2+}-comodified BNT-based lead-free solid solution, (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3, has been developed and prepared by a conventional ceramic technique, and its structure, dielectric and piezoelectric properties have been studied. The results of X-ray diffraction reveal that all the ceramics possess a pure perovskite structure and the MPB is formed at 0.04< y <0.10. After the substitutions of NdE^{3+} and BaE^{2+} for the A-site ions of the BNT lattices, the ceramics exhibit a relatively low E c and large P r, and thus improve significantly piezoelectric properties of the ceramics. Because of lower E c, larger P r and the MPB, the ceramics with x/y =0.02/0.06 possess the optimum piezoelectric properties: d 33 =150pC/N and k P =30.5%. The depolarization temperature T d shows a strong compositional dependences and the composition situated within the MPB possesses the lowest T d. The excess of NdE^{3+} leads to a decrease in T d. Relaxor behavior is observed in the (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3 ceramics. It is suggested that this should be resulted from the cation disordering in the 12-fold coordination sites. The ceramics also exhibit deformed or slim P\u2013E loops at high temperatures near/above T d, suggesting that the ceramics may contain both the polar and non-polar regions.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-containing piezoelectric materials with perovskite structure such as lead zirconate titanate (PZT) and PZT-based multi-component systems are widely used for piezoelectric actuators, sensors and transducers in various electronic devices due to their excellent piezoelectric properties. Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 (BNT) has a rhombohedral perovskite structure and is considered to be one of the promising alternatives to lead-free piezoelectric ceramics due to its strong ferroelectricity (P r =38\u03bcC/cmE^{2}) at room temperature. However, its coercive field is also high (E c =7.3kV/mm), making the polarization of the ceramic very difficult. Therefore, the pure BNT ceramic exhibits relatively poor piezoelectric properties (d 33 =58pC/N). A number of studies have been carried out to improve the poling process and enhance the piezoelectric properties of the ceramics; these include the formation of solid solutions of BNT with other ABO3-type ferroelectrics or non-ferroelectrics, e.g. BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , BNT\u2013Ba2NbO6 , BNT\u2013BiAlO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013SrTiO3 , BNT\u2013Ba(Cu1/2W1/2)O3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013BaTiO3 , and BNT\u2013K0.5Na0.5NbO3 , the substitutions of analogous ions for the A-site (Bi0.5Na0.5)E^{+} or B-site TiE^{4+} ions, e.g. (Bi0.5Na0.5)0.94Ba0.06Zr y Ti1\u2212 y O3 and (Bi1/2Na1/2)Ti1\u2212 x (Zn1/3Nb2/3) x O3 , and the doping of metal oxides, e.g. Ta-doped 0.94BNT\u20130.06BaTiO3 and La2O3-doped 0.92BNT\u20130.08BaTiO3. It can be seen that the formation of a new solid solution has been shown to be an effective method to improve the piezoelectric properties of the BNT\u2013based ceramics. It is hence anticipated that, by developing a new multi-component system with low E c and large P r, the piezoelectric properties of the BNT-based ceramics can be further enhanced. In the present work, a new lead-free solid solution, (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3 (abbreviated as BNNBT-x/y), was developed by the co-substitutions of 2\u20134mol% NdE^{3+} for BiE^{3+} and BaE^{2+} (2\u201312%) for (Bi0.5Na0.5)E^{2+} in the A-sites of BNT ceramics and fabricated by an ordinary sintering method, and its dielectric and piezoelectric properties were studied.

# CONCLUSION
A new NdE^{3+}- and BaE^{2+}-comodified BNT-based lead-free solid solution, (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3, has been developed and prepared by a conventional ceramic technique, and its structure, dielectric and piezoelectric properties have been studied. The results of X-ray diffraction reveal that all the ceramics possess a pure perovskite structure and the MPB is formed at 0.04< y <0.10. After the substitutions of NdE^{3+} and BaE^{2+} for the A-site ions of the BNT lattices, the ceramics exhibit a relatively low E c and large P r, and thus improve significantly piezoelectric properties of the ceramics. Because of lower E c, larger P r and the MPB, the ceramics with x/y =0.02/0.06 possess the optimum piezoelectric properties: d 33 =150pC/N and k P =30.5%. The depolarization temperature T d shows a strong compositional dependences and the composition situated within the MPB possesses the lowest T d. The excess of NdE^{3+} leads to a decrease in T d. Relaxor behavior is observed in the (Bi1\u2212 x \u2212 y Nd x Na1\u2212 y )0.5Ba y TiO3 ceramics. It is suggested that this should be resulted from the cation disordering in the 12-fold coordination sites. The ceramics also exhibit deformed or slim P\u2013E loops at high temperatures near/above T d, suggesting that the ceramics may contain both the polar and non-polar regions.
