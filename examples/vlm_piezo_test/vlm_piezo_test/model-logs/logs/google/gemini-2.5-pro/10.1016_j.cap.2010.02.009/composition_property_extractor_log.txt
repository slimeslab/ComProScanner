# INTRODUCTION
Pb(Zr,Ti)O3 (PZT) and PZT-based multi-component ceramics have been widely used in electromechanical devices due to their excellent piezoelectric properties. Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 (BNT) is a perovskite-structured ferroelectric with a rhombohedral symmetry. Because of its large remanent polarization (P r =38\u03bcC/cmE^{2}), BNT has been considered as a good candidate for lead-free piezoelectric materials. However, the high coercive field (E c =7.3kV/mm) makes poling of the ceramic difficult. As a result, the BNT ceramic usually exhibits weak piezoelectric coefficient (d 33 =83pC/N) , which is significantly lower than that of the Pb(Zr0.52Ti0.48)O3 ceramic (d 33 =223pC/N). To improve the piezoelectric properties, a number of BNT-based solid solutions, such as BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , CeO2-doped BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013NaNbO3 , (Bi0.5Na0.5)0.94Ba0.06Zr y Ti1\u2212 y O3 , BNT\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013BaTiO3\u2013SrTiO3 , Ta-doped BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , (Bi0.5Na0.5)1+ x TiO3 , Bi0.5(Na1\u2212 x \u2212 y K x Li y )0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013SrTiO3 and BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013BaTiO3 have been developed and studied intensively. Our previous works have shown that BNT\u2013BaTiO3\u2013SrTiO3 ceramics possess good piezoelectric properties and the substitution of LiE^{+} for NaE^{+} is effective in improving the piezoelectric properties and decreasing the sintering temperature of BNT-based ceramics. In the present work, a new BNT-based multi-component solid solution, (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3, is developed by partially substituting LiE^{+} for NaE^{+} in an A-site of 0.92BNT\u20130.06BaTiO3\u20130.02SrTiO3 ceramics.

# CONCLUSION
New multi-component lead-free ceramics (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3 have been fabricated by a conventional ceramic technique, and their ferroelectric, piezoelectric and dielectric properties have been studied. The results of X-ray diffraction reveal that LiE^{+}, BaE^{2+} and SrE^{2+} diffuse into the Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 lattices to form a solid solution with a pure perovskite structure. Because of the low E c and large P r, the (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3 ceramics with x =0.075\u20130.125 show the optimum piezoelectric properties: d 33 =189\u2013235pC/N, k P =33.6\u201336.3% and k t =51.6\u201354.3%. The ceramic with x =0.15 possesses poor piezoelectric properties because of the poor ferroelectric properties and the existence of the non-polar phase.

# INTRODUCTION
Pb(Zr,Ti)O3 (PZT) and PZT-based multi-component ceramics have been widely used in electromechanical devices due to their excellent piezoelectric properties. Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 (BNT) is a perovskite-structured ferroelectric with a rhombohedral symmetry. Because of its large remanent polarization (P r =38\u03bcC/cmE^{2}), BNT has been considered as a good candidate for lead-free piezoelectric materials. However, the high coercive field (E c =7.3kV/mm) makes poling of the ceramic difficult. As a result, the BNT ceramic usually exhibits weak piezoelectric coefficient (d 33 =83pC/N) , which is significantly lower than that of the Pb(Zr0.52Ti0.48)O3 ceramic (d 33 =223pC/N). To improve the piezoelectric properties, a number of BNT-based solid solutions, such as BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , CeO2-doped BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013NaNbO3 , (Bi0.5Na0.5)0.94Ba0.06Zr y Ti1\u2212 y O3 , BNT\u2013KNbO3 , BNT\u2013BaTiO3\u2013SrTiO3 , Ta-doped BNT\u2013BaTiO3 , (Bi0.5Na0.5)1+ x TiO3 , Bi0.5(Na1\u2212 x \u2212 y K x Li y )0.5TiO3 , BNT\u2013SrTiO3 and BNT\u2013Bi0.5K0.5TiO3\u2013BaTiO3 have been developed and studied intensively. Our previous works have shown that BNT\u2013BaTiO3\u2013SrTiO3 ceramics possess good piezoelectric properties and the substitution of LiE^{+} for NaE^{+} is effective in improving the piezoelectric properties and decreasing the sintering temperature of BNT-based ceramics. In the present work, a new BNT-based multi-component solid solution, (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3, is developed by partially substituting LiE^{+} for NaE^{+} in an A-site of 0.92BNT\u20130.06BaTiO3\u20130.02SrTiO3 ceramics.

# CONCLUSION
New multi-component lead-free ceramics (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3 have been fabricated by a conventional ceramic technique, and their ferroelectric, piezoelectric and dielectric properties have been studied. The results of X-ray diffraction reveal that LiE^{+}, BaE^{2+} and SrE^{2+} diffuse into the Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3 lattices to form a solid solution with a pure perovskite structure. Because of the low E c and large P r, the (Bi0.92Na0.92\u2212 x Li x )0.5Ba0.06Sr0.02TiO3 ceramics with x =0.075\u20130.125 show the optimum piezoelectric properties: d 33 =189\u2013235pC/N, k P =33.6\u201336.3% and k t =51.6\u201354.3%. The ceramic with x =0.15 possesses poor piezoelectric properties because of the poor ferroelectric properties and the existence of the non-polar phase.
