# TITLE
Synthesis and piezoelectric properties of [Bi1-z(Na 1-x-y-zKxLiy)]0.5Ba zTiO3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics

# ABSTRACT
[Bi(NaKLi)]BaTiO lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were fabricated by ordinary ceramic technique and the piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties of the ceramics were studied. The ceramics can be well sintered at 1,100–1,150 °C for 2 h. X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis shows that KE^{+}, LiE^{+} and BaE^{2+} diffuse into the BiNaTiO lattices to form a solid solution with a single-phase perovskite structure. The introduction of KE^{+}, LiE^{+} and BaE^{2+} into BiNaTiO significantly decreases the coercive field  but maintains the large remanent polarization  of the materials. The ceramics provide piezoelectric constant  of 205 pC/N, electromechanical coupling factor  of 0.346, remanent polarization  of 31.7–38.5 μC/cmE^{2}, and coercive field  of 3.18–5.16 kV/mm.

# INTRODUCTION
Pb(Ti,Zr)TiO3-based piezoelectric ceramics are widely used for various electronic and microelectronic devices. At present, alkali metal niobate-based, bismuth layer-structured-type, Bi0.5Na0.5TiO3-based (abbreviated as BNT) and BaTiO3-based ferroelectrics are known as lead-free piezoelectric ceramics. Among these materials, BNT is thought to be a strong candidate of lead-free piezoelectric ceramics because of its relatively large remanent polarization (Pr = 38 \u03bcC/cmE^{2})[]. However, because pure BNT ceramics have a high coercive field (Ec = 7.3 kV/mm) and large electric conductivity[], these ceramics are difficult to be completely polarized. In order to improve the piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties of BNT ceramics, some solid solutions of BNT with BaTiO3 , Bi0.5K0.5TiO3 , NaNbO3 , Ba(Cu1/2W1/2)O3 , BiFeO3  are extensively investigated. According to the characteristics of BNT ferroelectric, it is believed that a key point to further enhance the piezoelectric properties is to decrease the coercive field efficiently and simultaneously retain the strong ferroelectricity of BNT-based ceramics. Therefore, the research and development of new BNT-based systems with a larger Pr and lower Ec may be a promising way to obviously enhance the piezoelectric properties of BNT-based ceramics. In this paper, new [Bi1-z(Na1-x-y-zKxLiy)]0.5BazTiO3 (abbreviated as BNKLB-x/y/z) ceramics were prepared by the conventional ceramic processing and their microstructures and piezoelectric properties were studied.

# CONCLUSION
New [Bi1-z(Na1-x-y-zKxLiy)]0.5BazTiO3 ceramics were prepared by the conventional ceramic sintering processing and their microstructures and piezoelectric properties were studied. The introduction of KE^{+}, LiE^{+} and BaE^{2+} to the BNT lattice causes an evident change in the microstructure of the BNT ceramics and decreases coercive field greatly but large remanent polarization is simultaneously maintained. The ceramics exhibit piezoelectric constant d33 of 205 pC/N, electromechanical coupling factor kp of 0.346, remanent polarization Pr of 31.7\u201338.5 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}, and coercive field Ec of 3.18\u20135.16 kV/mm.
