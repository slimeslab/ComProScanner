# ABSTRACT
New lead-free piezoelectric ceramics of ABO perovskite type, BiNa[Ti(SbNb)]O, were synthesized by conventional solid state reaction. The effect of the replacement of complex ions of (SbNb)E^{4+} in the B cationic site on structural and electrical properties was investigated. The XRD analysis showed that all samples exhibited a single phase of perovskite structure. Piezoelectric and dielectric measurement revealed that the substitution of (SbNb)E^{4+} ions lead to an increase in piezoelectric constant(), electromechanical coupling factor(), relative dielectric constant(),and loss tangent (tan), while excess of (NbSb)E^{4+} ions results in a decrease in , , , but an increase in tan. At low frequency and high temperature, dielectric constant increased sharply attributed to the superparaelectric clusters after (SbNb)E^{4+} ions substitution.

# INTRODUCTION
The most widely used piezoelectric materials are Pb(Zr, Ti)O3(PZT)-based three component system. Therefore, it is necessary to develop environment-friendly lead-free piezoelectric ceramics to replace PZT based ceramics. Among the lead-free compounds, bismuth sodium titanate (Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3, abbreviate as BNT), is interesting as BNT shows a strong ferroelectricity and high Curie temperature Tc = 320 \xb0C. This material belongs to the peroskite family ABO3, which has drawbacks such as high conductivity and large coercive field of 73 kV/cm, to cause problems in poling process. As a result of those, a lot of work to modify and improve piezoelectric properties of BNT ceramics have been done by substitution of BaTiO3 , K1/2Bi1/2TiO3 , NaNbO3, , BiFeO3 , La2O3. Therefore, the present work was to prepare and to characterize Bi1/2Na1/2[Ti1\u2212x(Sb1/2Nb1/2)x]O3 ceramics of perovskite type derived from Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 by complex ions of compensation valent substitutions. The study mainly describes the complex ions of (Sb1/2Nb1/2)E^{4+} substitution on improving the electrical properties of BNT ceramic.

# CONCLUSION
Lead-free piezoelectric ceramics Bi1/2Na1/2[Ti1\u2212x(Sb1/2Nb1/2)x]O3, a new member of the BNT-based group, was prepared by conventional solid state reaction and their dielectric, piezoelectric properties were investigated. The piezoelectric constant d33 and the electromechanical coupling factor kt increased with the increasing content of (Sb1/2Nb1/2)E^{4+}, showed the maximum value of 97 pC/N and 0.50 at x = 0.8 %, respectively, and rapidly decreased when x over 0.8 %. The loss tangent tan\u03b4 linearly increased with the increasing amount of (Sb1/2Nb1/2)E^{4+}. Temperature dependence of dielectric constant \u025br measurement indicated these compounds were relaxor ferroelectric. At low frequency and high temperature, dielectric constant increased sharply attributed to the superparaelectric clusters after (Sb1/2Nb1/2)E^{4+} ions substitution.
