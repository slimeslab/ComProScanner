# TITLE
Phase transition, electrical and luminescent properties of Dy-doped K0.5Na0.5NbO3-based lead-free ceramics

# ABSTRACT
Dy-modified KNaNbO-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics of (KNaLi)Dy(NbTaSb)O lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were prepared by a conventional ceramics technique and the effects of DyE^{3+} doping on the crystal structure, microstructure, dielectric, piezoelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were investigated. The ceramics with low Dy levels (x ≤ 0.015) show a pure perovskite structure, while a small amount of secondary phase DyNbO can be detected in the ceramics with x ≥ 0.02. The increase in the level of DyE^{3+} doping leads to the shift of crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic–tetragonal phases to pseudo-cubic phase. The addition of DyE^{3+} inhibits the grain growth of the ceramics and induces the diffusive phase transition in the ceramics with high levels of DyE^{3+} ions. The piezoelectric constant  of the ceramics decreases from 198 to 52 pC/N with x increasing from 0 to 0.03 because of the shift of the crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic–tetragonal phase to pseudo-cubic phase. The Dy-doped ceramics excited at 453 nm have excellent luminescent properties. Two strong emission spectral bands centered at 476 and 573 nm are observed, corresponding to the transition of the E^{4}F → E^{6}H and E^{4}F → E^{6}H levels of DyE^{3+}, respectively. Our study shows that the ceramic with x = 0.015 possesses simultaneously strong ferroelectricity, good piezoelectricity and excellent luminescence, suggesting that the Dy-doped ceramics may have potential application in multifunctional devices.

# INTRODUCTION
In recent years, K0.5Na0.5NbO3 (KNN)-based ceramics have attracted considerable attention because of their excellent ferroelectric properties and high Curie temperature. It has been considered as one of the most promising candidates for lead-free ceramics [\u2013]. However, because of the high volatility of the alkaline metal elements during sintering process, the pure KNN-based ceramics exhibit poor piezoelectricity. In order to improve the piezoelectricity of the ceramics, a number of investigations have been carried out, including the two-step sintering  and the substitution of analogous ions (e.g., LiE^{+}, AgE^{+}, TaE^{5+}, SbE^{5+}, etc.) for A-sites ions and/or B-sites ions [\u2013]. Our previous study has shown that the (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 ceramic exhibits excellent ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties because of the coexistence of orthorhombic and tetragonal phases. In addition, as a donor to KNN, it is expected that the introduction of rare earth ions into KNN would improve the properties of the piezoelectric ceramics. It has been noted that the rare earth ion of DyE^{3+} has been used widely in the optics area and is very suitable for application on illumination materials due to its excellent luminescent properties [\u2013]. Moreover, the radius of DyE^{3+} (0.912 \xc5) is similar with the LiE^{+} (0.76 \xc5), NaE^{+} (0.97 \xc5) and KE^{+} (1.33 \xc5), which makes DyE^{3+} substitute the A-site ions of the KNN-based ceramics as a donor. To our knowledge, there is little work on the effect of the DyE^{3+} doping on the electrical properties and luminescent properties of (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 (KNLNST) ceramics. In the present work, (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)1\u2212xDyx/3(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were prepared by an ordinary sintering technique, and the piezoelectric, ferroelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were investigated.

# CONCLUSION
Dy-doped KNLNST lead-free multifunctional ceramics were successfully prepared by the conventional solid state method, and their structure, dielectric, piezoelectric, ferroelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were studied. The increase in the level of DyE^{3+} doping leads to the shift of crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic\u2013tetragonal phase to pseudo-cubic phase, which degrades the piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties of the materials. Upon DyE^{3+} ions doping, the grain growth is inhibited, Curie temperature (TC) is suppressed, and the ferroelectric and paraelectric phase transition becomes more diffusive. Under the excitation of 453 nm, ceramics with DyE^{3+} exhibit two emission peaks, corresponding to the E^{4}F9/2 \u2192 E^{6}H15/2 and E^{4}F9/2 \u2192 E^{6}H13/2, respectively and the transition is mainly magnetic dipole transition. The ceramic with x = 0.015 exhibits the strongest emission intensities and possesses simultaneously strong ferroelectricity with the Pr of 11.6 \u03bcC/cmE^{2} and good piezoelectricity with the d33 of 125 pC/N, showing a potential application in multifunctional devices.

# TITLE
Phase transition, electrical and luminescent properties of Dy-doped K0.5Na0.5NbO3-based lead-free ceramics

# ABSTRACT
Dy-modified KNaNbO-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics of (KNaLi)Dy(NbTaSb)O lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were prepared by a conventional ceramics technique and the effects of DyE^{3+} doping on the crystal structure, microstructure, dielectric, piezoelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were investigated. The ceramics with low Dy levels (x ≤ 0.015) show a pure perovskite structure, while a small amount of secondary phase DyNbO can be detected in the ceramics with x ≥ 0.02. The increase in the level of DyE^{3+} doping leads to the shift of crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic–tetragonal phases to pseudo-cubic phase. The addition of DyE^{3+} inhibits the grain growth of the ceramics and induces the diffusive phase transition in the ceramics with high levels of DyE^{3+} ions. The piezoelectric constant  of the ceramics decreases from 198 to 52 pC/N with x increasing from 0 to 0.03 because of the shift of the crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic–tetragonal phase to pseudo-cubic phase. The Dy-doped ceramics excited at 453 nm have excellent luminescent properties. Two strong emission spectral bands centered at 476 and 573 nm are observed, corresponding to the transition of the E^{4}F → E^{6}H and E^{4}F → E^{6}H levels of DyE^{3+}, respectively. Our study shows that the ceramic with x = 0.015 possesses simultaneously strong ferroelectricity, good piezoelectricity and excellent luminescence, suggesting that the Dy-doped ceramics may have potential application in multifunctional devices.

# INTRODUCTION
In recent years, K0.5Na0.5NbO3 (KNN)-based ceramics have attracted considerable attention because of their excellent ferroelectric properties and high Curie temperature. It has been considered as one of the most promising candidates for lead-free ceramics [\u2013]. However, because of the high volatility of the alkaline metal elements during sintering process, the pure KNN-based ceramics exhibit poor piezoelectricity. In order to improve the piezoelectricity of the ceramics, a number of investigations have been carried out, including the two-step sintering  and the substitution of analogous ions (e.g., LiE^{+}, AgE^{+}, TaE^{5+}, SbE^{5+}, etc.) for A-sites ions and/or B-sites ions [\u2013]. Our previous study has shown that the (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 ceramic exhibits excellent ferroelectric and piezoelectric properties because of the coexistence of orthorhombic and tetragonal phases. In addition, as a donor to KNN, it is expected that the introduction of rare earth ions into KNN would improve the properties of the piezoelectric ceramics. It has been noted that the rare earth ion of DyE^{3+} has been used widely in the optics area and is very suitable for application on illumination materials due to its excellent luminescent properties [\u2013]. Moreover, the radius of DyE^{3+} (0.912 \xc5) is similar with the LiE^{+} (0.76 \xc5), NaE^{+} (0.97 \xc5) and KE^{+} (1.33 \xc5), which makes DyE^{3+} substitute the A-site ions of the KNN-based ceramics as a donor. To our knowledge, there is little work on the effect of the DyE^{3+} doping on the electrical properties and luminescent properties of (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 (KNLNST) ceramics. In the present work, (K0.48Na0.48Li0.04)1\u2212xDyx/3(Nb0.90Ta0.04Sb0.06)O3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were prepared by an ordinary sintering technique, and the piezoelectric, ferroelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were investigated.

# CONCLUSION
Dy-doped KNLNST lead-free multifunctional ceramics were successfully prepared by the conventional solid state method, and their structure, dielectric, piezoelectric, ferroelectric and luminescent properties of the ceramics were studied. The increase in the level of DyE^{3+} doping leads to the shift of crystal structure of the ceramics from the coexistence of orthorhombic\u2013tetragonal phase to pseudo-cubic phase, which degrades the piezoelectric and ferroelectric properties of the materials. Upon DyE^{3+} ions doping, the grain growth is inhibited, Curie temperature (TC) is suppressed, and the ferroelectric and paraelectric phase transition becomes more diffusive. Under the excitation of 453 nm, ceramics with DyE^{3+} exhibit two emission peaks, corresponding to the E^{4}F9/2 \u2192 E^{6}H15/2 and E^{4}F9/2 \u2192 E^{6}H13/2, respectively and the transition is mainly magnetic dipole transition. The ceramic with x = 0.015 exhibits the strongest emission intensities and possesses simultaneously strong ferroelectricity with the Pr of 11.6 \u03bcC/cmE^{2} and good piezoelectricity with the d33 of 125 pC/N, showing a potential application in multifunctional devices.
