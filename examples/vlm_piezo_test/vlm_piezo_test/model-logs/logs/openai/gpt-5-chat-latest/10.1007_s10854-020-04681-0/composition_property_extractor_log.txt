# ABSTRACT
In this study, CaE^{2+}, ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} were simultaneously doped at A-sites and B-sites, respectively, to clarify the structure and enhance the electrical performance. (BaCa)(TiZrSn)O (BCTZS) lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were successfully fabricated by conventional solid-state reactive sintering method. The effects of CaE^{2+} content () and sintering temperature on microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of BCTZS were investigated. # INTRODUCTION
Lead-based ceramics, such as Pb(Zr1\u2212xTix)O3 (PZT), are the mainstream in the market due to their superior piezoelectric and dielectric properties , however, commercial manufacturing and applications of these ceramics will be restricted because of the toxicity for human health and environment [, , , , , , \u2013]. Material chemists are trying to explore lead-free piezoelectric ceramics to replace them, among which BaTiO3 (BT), (K, Na)NbO3, (Bi1/2Na1/2)TiO3 and BiFeO3-based ceramics have attracted much attention [, , , \u2013]. As the earliest discovered piezoelectric ceramics, BT is now mostly used as a dielectric material rather than a piezoelectric material, because of its low Curie temperature (TC = 120 \xb0C) and inferior electrical properties with a piezoelectric coefficient d33 = 191 pC NE^{\u22121}. Recently, an increasing attention has been paid to improve the electrical properties of BT-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics by ion doping, optimization preparing methods and so on. Ion doping (such as doping CaE^{2+} in A-sites and ZrE^{4+} and/or SnE^{4+} or other cations in B-sites) can change phase transition temperatures of BT to construct two or multiphase coexisting structure near room temperature leading to the enhanced electrical properties. Li et al. obtained the d33 = 440 pC NE^{\u22121} at the composition of (Ba0.97Ca0.03)(Ti0.94Sn0.06)O3 ceramic composed of orthorhombic (O) and tetragonal (T) phases. The d33 of (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 and (Ba0.945Ca0.055)(Ti0.91Sn0.09)O3 ceramics was 568 pC NE^{\u22121} and 630 pC NE^{\u22121}, respectively, by forming O\u2013T phases structure [, , ]. Liu reported the d33 = 620 pC NE^{\u22121} at the composition of (Ba0.85Ca0.15)(Ti0.9Zr0.1)O3 by constructing the coexistence of O\u2013T phases. (Ba0.94Ca0.06)(Ti0.925Zr0.075)O3 consisted of O-rhombohedral (R) phases reached d33 = 360 pC NE^{\u22121} [, ]. The coexistence of R-pseudocubic (PC)\u2013O phases contributed to the high d33 = 670 pC NE^{\u22121} for (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.89Sn0.11)O3 ceramics. A comprehensive analysis on the coupling role of co-doping ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} in BaTiO3 ceramics on the phase transition between R, O, T and cubic (C) phases on a simple composition Ba(Ti0.94Sn0.06\u2212xZrx)O3 has been reported, and the better electrical performances obtained by collaborating ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} content at Ba(Ti0.94Zr0.01Sn0.05)O3 ceramic with the values d33 = 402 pC NE^{\u22121} and kp = 39%. However, there are still lack of reports on BT ceramics with simultaneously co-doped CaE^{2+}, ZrE^{4+}, SnE^{4+} at both A and B-sites, which motivates us to develop such complex ceramics to have a better understanding on their structures and electrical performances. In this study, the phase structure, microstructure and electrical performances of (Ba1\u2212xCax)(Ti0.94Zr0.01Sn0.05)O3 (BCxTZS) ceramics were investigated with an especial emphasis on the effects of CaE^{2+} content (x) and sintering temperature (Ts).

# CONCLUSION
In summary, BCxTZS (0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08) ceramics were successfully prepared by solid -state reactive sintering method, whose phase structure and electrical properties were comprehensively studied for all compositions. Doping CaE^{2+} into A-sites of BTZS ceramics can regulate phase structure of BCxTZS (0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08) ceramics: BCxTZS is the coexistence of R\u2013O phases at 0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.02 and it becomes O\u2013T phases as 0.02 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, whose ratio of O-phase drops from 73 to 8% when x increases from 0.05 to 0.08. Proper phase ratio (CT/CO = 67/33) and big grain size contribute to the best electrical properties d33 = 442 pC NE^{\u22121}, kp = 43% and Qm = 112 at x = 0.07. The excellent electrical performances with the values d33 = 511 pC NE^{\u22121}, kp = 43% and Qm = 126 were achieved by optimizing the sintering temperatures of the best performed composition BC0.07TZS. The superior d33 along with comparable Qm with PZT ceramics, indicates that BCxTZS ceramics are promising lead-free piezoelectrical ceramics.

# TITLE
Enhanced electrical properties of lead-free (Ba1−xCa x)(Ti0.94Zr0.01Sn0.05)O3 piezoelectric ceramics by optimizing phase ratio and sintering temperature

# ABSTRACT
In this study, CaE^{2+}, ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} were simultaneously doped at A-sites and B-sites, respectively, to clarify the structure and enhance the electrical performance. (BaCa)(TiZrSn)O (BCTZS) lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were successfully fabricated by conventional solid-state reactive sintering method. The effects of CaE^{2+} content () and sintering temperature on microstructure, phase structure and electrical properties of BCTZS were investigated. The superior  along with the comparable  with PZT ceramics indicates that BCTZS ceramics are promising lead-free piezoelectric ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-based ceramics, such as Pb(Zr1\u2212xTix)O3 (PZT), are the mainstream in the market due to their superior piezoelectric and dielectric properties , however, commercial manufacturing and applications of these ceramics will be restricted because of the toxicity for human health and environment [, , , , , , \u2013]. Material chemists are trying to explore lead-free piezoelectric ceramics to replace them, among which BaTiO3 (BT), (K, Na)NbO3, (Bi1/2Na1/2)TiO3 and BiFeO3-based ceramics have attracted much attention [, , , \u2013]. As the earliest discovered piezoelectric ceramics, BT is now mostly used as a dielectric material rather than a piezoelectric material, because of its low Curie temperature (TC = 120 \xb0C) and inferior electrical properties with a piezoelectric coefficient d33 = 191 pC NE^{\u22121}. Recently, an increasing attention has been paid to improve the electrical properties of BT-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics by ion doping, optimization preparing methods and so on. Ion doping (such as doping CaE^{2+} in A-sites and ZrE^{4+} and/or SnE^{4+} or other cations in B-sites) can change phase transition temperatures of BT to construct two or multiphase coexisting structure near room temperature leading to the enhanced electrical properties. Li et al. obtained the d33 = 440 pC NE^{\u22121} at the composition of (Ba0.97Ca0.03)(Ti0.94Sn0.06)O3 ceramic composed of orthorhombic (O) and tetragonal (T) phases. The d33 of (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 and (Ba0.945Ca0.055)(Ti0.91Sn0.09)O3 ceramics was 568 pC NE^{\u22121} and 630 pC NE^{\u22121}, respectively, by forming O\u2013T phases structure [, , ]. Liu reported the d33 = 620 pC NE^{\u22121} at the composition of (Ba0.85Ca0.15)(Ti0.9Zr0.1)O3 by constructing the coexistence of O\u2013T phases. (Ba0.94Ca0.06)(Ti0.925Zr0.075)O3 consisted of O-rhombohedral (R) phases reached d33 = 360 pC NE^{\u22121} [, ]. The coexistence of R-pseudocubic (PC)\u2013O phases contributed to the high d33 = 670 pC NE^{\u22121} for (Ba0.95Ca0.05)(Ti0.89Sn0.11)O3 ceramics. A comprehensive analysis on the coupling role of co-doping ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} in BaTiO3 ceramics on the phase transition between R, O, T and cubic (C) phases on a simple composition Ba(Ti0.94Sn0.06\u2212xZrx)O3 has been reported, and the better electrical performances obtained by collaborating ZrE^{4+} and SnE^{4+} content at Ba(Ti0.94Zr0.01Sn0.05)O3 ceramic with the values d33 = 402 pC NE^{\u22121} and kp = 39%. However, there are still lack of reports on BT ceramics with simultaneously co-doped CaE^{2+}, ZrE^{4+}, SnE^{4+} at both A and B-sites, which motivates us to develop such complex ceramics to have a better understanding on their structures and electrical performances. In this study, the phase structure, microstructure and electrical performances of (Ba1\u2212xCax)(Ti0.94Zr0.01Sn0.05)O3 (BCxTZS) ceramics were investigated with an especial emphasis on the effects of CaE^{2+} content (x) and sintering temperature (Ts).

# CONCLUSION
In summary, BCxTZS (0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08) ceramics were successfully prepared by solid -state reactive sintering method, whose phase structure and electrical properties were comprehensively studied for all compositions. Doping CaE^{2+} into A-sites of BTZS ceramics can regulate phase structure of BCxTZS (0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08) ceramics: BCxTZS is the coexistence of R\u2013O phases at 0.01 \u2264 x \u2264 0.02 and it becomes O\u2013T phases as 0.02 \u2264 x \u2264 0.08, whose ratio of O-phase drops from 73 to 8% when x increases from 0.05 to 0.08. Proper phase ratio (CT/CO = 67/33) and big grain size contribute to the best electrical properties d33 = 442 pC NE^{\u22121}, kp = 43% and Qm = 112 at x = 0.07. The excellent electrical performances with the values d33 = 511 pC NE^{\u22121}, kp = 43% and Qm = 126 were achieved by optimizing the sintering temperatures of the best performed composition BC0.07TZS. The superior d33 along with comparable Qm with PZT ceramics, indicates that BCxTZS ceramics are promising lead-free piezoelectrical ceramics.
