# INTRODUCTION
Pb(Zr,Ti)O3 and Pb(Zr,Ti)O3-based multi-component ceramics with a perovskite structure are widely used in actuators, sensors as well as microelectronic devices because of their superior electrical properties. K0.5Na0.5NbO3 (KNN), a solid solution of ferroelectric KNbO3 and antiferroelectric NaNbO3, is considered as a good candidate for lead-free piezoelectric ceramics because of its high Curie temperature (\u223c420 \xb0C), good ferroelectric properties (Pr = 33 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}) and large electromechanical coupling factors [, ]. However, because of the high volatility of alkaline elements at high temperatures, it is very difficult to obtain dense and well-sintered KNN ceramics using ordinary sintering processes. Previously, a number of investigations have been carried out to improve the sintering performance and electrical properties of KNN ceramics, including the formation of solid solutions of KNN with other ABO3-type compounds, e.g. Li(Nb,Ta,Sb)O3[], CaTiO3 , BaTiO3 , SrTiO3 , LiTaO3 , LiSbO3 , the application of special fabrication techniques, e.g. cold-isostatic pressing , high-energy milling , spark plasma sintering , templated grain growth  and hot pressing , and the use of sintering aids, e.g. CuO  and MnO2. On the other hand, very recently, it has been proved that AgNbO3, an antiferroelectric at room temperature, has an extremely large polarization (52 \u03bcC/mE^{2}) and electromechanical response. In the present work, a new lead-free solid solution KNN-AgNbO3 was formed by introducing AgNbO3 into KNN and prepared in air by a simple pressureless processing without sintering aids, cold isostatic pressing or special power handing.

# CONCLUSION
New (K0.5Na0.5)1\u2212xAgxNbO3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics have been successfully prepared by a conventional ceramic fabrication technique and their electrical properties have been studied. From the results of XRD, the ceramics possess a perovskite structure with an orthorhombic symmetry, indicating that AgE^{+} diffuses into the K0.5Na0.5NbO3 lattices to form a new solid solution with an orthorhombic perovskite structure. After the addition of AgNbO3 to KNN, both paraelectric cubic-ferroelectric tetragonal (TC) and ferroelectric tetragonal-ferroelectric orthorhombic phase transition temperatures (TO-T) decrease slightly. The ceramics with high AgE^{+} concentration (x > 0.16) exhibit the weak ferroelectricity because of the existence of a second phase. The electrical properties of the (K0.5Na0.5)0.90Ag0.10NbO3 ceramics are found to be optimum: d33 = 135 pC/N, kP = 0.43, kt = 0.46, \u03b5r = 470, tan\u03b4 = 3.39%, and TC = 394 \xb0C.

# INTRODUCTION

K0.5Na0.5NbO3 (KNN), a solid solution of ferroelectric KNbO3 and antiferroelectric NaNbO3, is considered as a good candidate for lead-free piezoelectric ceramics because of its high Curie temperature (\u223c420 \xb0C), good ferroelectric properties (Pr = 33 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}) and large electromechanical coupling factors [, ].
