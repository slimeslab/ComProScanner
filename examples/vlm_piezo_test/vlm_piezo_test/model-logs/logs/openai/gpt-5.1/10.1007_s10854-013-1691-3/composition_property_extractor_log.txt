# TITLE
Properties of B-site non-stoichiometric (K0.5Na 0.5)(Nb0.9Ta0.1)1+x O3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics

# ABSTRACT
The non-stoichiometric (KNa)(NbTa)O ( = 0, ±0.005, ±0.010) [KN(NT)] lead-free piezoelectric ceramics were prepared by normal sintering. All the KN(NT) ceramics possess orthorhombic perovskite structure. The grain growth of the ceramics is inhibited and the relative density is improved with increasing . 0.5 mol% (NT)E^{5+} excess KN(NT) ceramic which sintered at 1,120 °C has the highest piezoelectric performances among with other samples. Meanwhile, the (NT)E^{5+} excess ceramics have better time stability than the (NT)E^{5+} deficient ones. These results show that the KN(NT) ceramic with  = 0.005 is a promising lead-free piezoelectric material.

# INTRODUCTION
Pb(Zr, Ti)O3 (PZT) based piezoelectric ceramics are widely used because they have good piezoelectric properties. However, the PZT based ceramics contain high content of toxic lead and are bringing health and environment concerns aggravated with their expanded applications. The best way to solve this problem is to search lead-free piezoelectric ceramic materials which will replace the lead-based materials [\u2013]. The composition (K0.5Na0.5)NbO3 (KNN) has been attracted much attention and considered as one of the most potential candidate for the replacement of PZT ceramics. However, a few drawbacks still prevent wide scale industrial use of KNN ceramics owing to the volatility of the alkali species at high temperatures. In order to improve the densification and the electrical properties of the KNN ceramics, many KNN-based systems have been investigated [\u2013]. It is known that the crystal structure, microstructure and electrical properties can be affected profoundly by a small deviation from stoichiometry in the KNN-based ceramics. Up to now, many researches on the A-site non-stoichiometric composition ceramics have been tried to improve piezoelectric properties of KNN-based ceramics [\u2013]. However, few researches on the B-site non-stoichiometric KNN-based compositions are systematically studied. Therefore, in this paper, we systematically investigate the phase structure, microstructure, and piezoelectric properties of B-site non-stoichiometric (K0.5Na0.5)(Nb0.9Ta0.1)1+xO3 ceramics.

# CONCLUSION
B-site non-stoichiometric (K0.5Na0.5)(Nb0.9Ta0.1)1+xO3 ceramics were manufactured by conventional solid state method. The influences of deficient or excess amount of (NT)E^{5+} on structure and electrical properties are investigated. 0.5 mol% (NT)E^{5+} excess KN(NT)1+x ceramics sintered at 1,120 \xb0C show the optimum piezoelectric performances such as piezoelectric constant of 167 pC/N, planar electromechanical coupling coefficient of 0.41, and dielectric loss of 3.2 %. Piezoelectric properties of the B-site excess ceramics show no obvious reduction when exposed in air for 30 days. The results may be instructive for further design and development of new KNN-based piezoelectric systems.
