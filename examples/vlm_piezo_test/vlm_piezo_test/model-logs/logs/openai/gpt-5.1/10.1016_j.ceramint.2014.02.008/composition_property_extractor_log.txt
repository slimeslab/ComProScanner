# INTRODUCTION
It is well known that lead zirconate titanate (PZT) ceramics have been widely used owing to the excellent properties induced by morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) where rhombohedral and tetragonal phases coexist. BaTiO3 is considered to be an excellent candidate for lead-free piezoelectric ceramics. However, the piezoelectric constant d 33 of BaTiO3 is relatively low (about 190pC/N), and the pure BaTiO3 undergoes a series of phase transformations with increasing temperature: rhombohedral to orthorhombic phase (R\u2192O) at \u221280\xb0C, orthorhombic to tetragonal phase (O\u2192T) at 5\xb0C, and finally tetragonal to cubic phase (T\u2192C) at 120\xb0C. Some attempts have been conducted to shift the phase transitions to room temperature and improve d 33 value through composition design. For example, the phase transformation temperature from tetragonal to orthorhombic (T T\u2013O) and orthorhombic to rhombohedral (T O\u2013R) can be shifted to room temperature by replacing TiE^{4+} with ZrE^{4+} at the B-site in the BaTiO3\u2013BaZrO3 system. The A-site replacement of BaE^{2+} by CaE^{2+} can reduce the T T\u2013O and T O\u2013R in the BaTiO3\u2013CaTiO3 system. Recently, Liu and Ren designed a ferroelectric system (1\u2212x)Ba(Zr0.2Ti0.8)O3\u2013x(Ba0.7Ca0.3)TiO3 (BZT\u2013xBCT) composition to a triple point and observed a very high d 33 value ~620pC/N. It was considered that BZT\u2013xBCT showed a phase diagram similar to the Pb-based systems, with a MPB starting from a triple point of a paraelectric cubic phase, ferroelectric rhombohedral and tetragonal. In addition, Li et al. reported (Ba,Ca)(Ti,Sn)O3 ceramic system with a relatively high d 33 of 530pC/N owing to the formation of polymorphic phase transition (PPT) from orthorhombic to tetragonal phase. It is known that the MPB or PPT plays a very important role in piezoelectric ceramics because dielectric and piezoelectric properties show maximum values around MPB and PPT. However, the MPB or PPT in BaTiO3-based system is very different from that in PZT-based ceramics. The piezoelectric constant shows very strong temperature dependence in BaTiO3-based system, which is often undesirable for practical application in any device. SrTiO3 is an effective additive being widely used in many perovskite piezoelectric ceramics to tailor the electrical performances. As well known, the radius of SrE^{2+} is 1.12\xc5, CaE^{2+} is 0.99\xc5, and BaE^{2+} is 1.34\xc5. In view of the radius, it is possible for SrE^{2+} to enter into the A-site of (Ba,Ca)(Ti,Sn)O3 ceramics system and affect the phase structure and electrical properties. Thus, a question can be proposed whether the electrical performance and temperature stability have been improved by modified phase transition behavior using the SrE^{2+} ion substitution. In this work, based on the modified phase transition behavior with ion doping, we designed a lead-free piezoelectric (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 system. The conventional solid state reaction method was employed to prepare designed (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 ceramics, and the effects of SrE^{2+} content on the phase transition, electrical properties and temperature stability were investigated in detail.

# CONCLUSION
(Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics have been successfully prepared by conventional sintering method. The phase structure, electrical properties and temperature dependence of (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free ceramics as a function of SrE^{2+} content were investigated. The phase transition of rhombohedral\u2013orthorhombic and orthorhombic\u2013tetragonal emerged to near room temperature with adjusting the SrE^{2+}content. Owing to the coexistence of orthorhombic and tetragonal phases at room temperature, outstanding electrical properties of d 33=583pC/N, k p =40%, P r =8.73 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}, E c =0.07kV/cm and electrical strain=0.20% were achieved in the (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08) O3 ceramics with x=0.01. Furthermore, the temperature stability results revealed that both d 33 and k p show good temperature stabilities in the range of \u22125 to 55\xb0C. The results indicated that SrE^{2+} substitution is an effective way to enhance the electric properties and temperature dependence of (Ba0.91Ca0.08Sr0.01)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 ceramics, which are promising as a lead-free piezoelectric candidate.

# INTRODUCTION
It is well known that lead zirconate titanate (PZT) ceramics have been widely used owing to the excellent properties induced by morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) where rhombohedral and tetragonal phases coexist. BaTiO3 is considered to be an excellent candidate for lead-free piezoelectric ceramics. However, the piezoelectric constant d 33 of BaTiO3 is relatively low (about 190pC/N), and the pure BaTiO3 undergoes a series of phase transformations with increasing temperature: rhombohedral to orthorhombic phase (R\u2192O) at \u221280\xb0C, orthorhombic to tetragonal phase (O\u2192T) at 5\xb0C, and finally tetragonal to cubic phase (T\u2192C) at 120\xb0C. Some attempts have been conducted to shift the phase transitions to room temperature and improve d 33 value through composition design. For example, the phase transformation temperature from tetragonal to orthorhombic (T T\u2013O) and orthorhombic to rhombohedral (T O\u2013R) can be shifted to room temperature by replacing TiE^{4+} with ZrE^{4+} at the B-site in the BaTiO3\u2013BaZrO3 system. The A-site replacement of BaE^{2+} by CaE^{2+} can reduce the T T\u2013O and T O\u2013R in the BaTiO3\u2013CaTiO3 system. Recently, Liu and Ren designed a ferroelectric system (1\u2212x)Ba(Zr0.2Ti0.8)O3\u2013x(Ba0.7Ca0.3)TiO3 (BZT\u2013xBCT) composition to a triple point and observed a very high d 33 value ~620pC/N. It was considered that BZT\u2013xBCT showed a phase diagram similar to the Pb-based systems, with a MPB starting from a triple point of a paraelectric cubic phase, ferroelectric rhombohedral and tetragonal. In addition, Li et al. reported (Ba,Ca)(Ti,Sn)O3 ceramic system with a relatively high d 33 of 530pC/N owing to the formation of polymorphic phase transition (PPT) from orthorhombic to tetragonal phase. It is known that the MPB or PPT plays a very important role in piezoelectric ceramics because dielectric and piezoelectric properties show maximum values around MPB and PPT. However, the MPB or PPT in BaTiO3-based system is very different from that in PZT-based ceramics. The piezoelectric constant shows very strong temperature dependence in BaTiO3-based system, which is often undesirable for practical application in any device. SrTiO3 is an effective additive being widely used in many perovskite piezoelectric ceramics to tailor the electrical performances. As well known, the radius of SrE^{2+} is 1.12\xc5, CaE^{2+} is 0.99\xc5, and BaE^{2+} is 1.34\xc5. In view of the radius, it is possible for SrE^{2+} to enter into the A-site of (Ba,Ca)(Ti,Sn)O3 ceramics system and affect the phase structure and electrical properties. Thus, a question can be proposed whether the electrical performance and temperature stability have been improved by modified phase transition behavior using the SrE^{2+} ion substitution. In this work, based on the modified phase transition behavior with ion doping, we designed a lead-free piezoelectric (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 system. The conventional solid state reaction method was employed to prepare designed (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 ceramics, and the effects of SrE^{2+} content on the phase transition, electrical properties and temperature stability were investigated in detail.

# CONCLUSION
(Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free piezoelectric ceramics have been successfully prepared by conventional sintering method. The phase structure, electrical properties and temperature dependence of (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 lead-free ceramics as a function of SrE^{2+} content were investigated. The phase transition of rhombohedral\u2013orthorhombic and orthorhombic\u2013tetragonal emerged to near room temperature with adjusting the SrE^{2+}content. Owing to the coexistence of orthorhombic and tetragonal phases at room temperature, outstanding electrical properties of d 33=583pC/N, k p =40%, P r =8.73 \u03bcC/cmE^{2}, E c =0.07kV/cm and electrical strain=0.20% were achieved in the (Ba0.91Ca0.09\u2212x Sr x )(Ti0.92Sn0.08) O3 ceramics with x=0.01. Furthermore, the temperature stability results revealed that both d 33 and k p show good temperature stabilities in the range of \u22125 to 55\xb0C. The results indicated that SrE^{2+} substitution is an effective way to enhance the electric properties and temperature dependence of (Ba0.91Ca0.08Sr0.01)(Ti0.92Sn0.08)O3 ceramics, which are promising as a lead-free piezoelectric candidate.
