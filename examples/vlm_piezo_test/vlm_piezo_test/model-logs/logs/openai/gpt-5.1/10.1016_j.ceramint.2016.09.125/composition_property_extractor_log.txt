# TITLE
The effect of B-site substitution on the structural evolution and electrical properties of lead-free (K,Na)NbO3 ceramics

# ABSTRACT
Lead-free (K0.49Na0.51)(Nb1−xSbx)O3 piezoelectric ceramics were prepared via the conventional sintering method and the effect of the substitution of Nb with Sb on the phase structure, microstructure and electrical properties of the prepared (K0.49Na0.51)(Nb1−xSbx)O3 ceramics was systematically investigated. The prepared ceramics exhibited a single-phase perovskite structure which changed from a standard orthorhombic structure to a pseudocubic structure as x was increased from 0 to 0.1. Substituting an appropriate amount of Sb5+ for Nb5+ was found to improve the microstructure and thereby enhance the piezoelectric/ferroelectric properties. The peak values of the piezoelectric constant d
                     33 (182 pC/N) and the electromechanical coupling coefficient k
                     p (41%) were obtained for the ceramic with x=0.06.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-free (K0.5Na0.5)NbO3 (KNN)-based piezoceramics have attracted much attention since the breakthrough reported by Saito et al. and are regarded as one of the most promising candidates for replacing (Pb, Zr)TiO3 ceramics. However, the piezoelectric constant d 33 of KNN is pretty low. For KNN ceramics fabricated via conventional sintering, a d 33 of about 100 pC/N was reported, which could be increased to the highest currently reported value of around 160 pC/N when changing the fabrication technique to hot pressing. Researchers have made tremendous efforts to improve the properties of KNN-based ceramics through optimizing their composition. Recently, due to its high Pauling electronegativity, Sb has been used to improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of KNN-based ceramics. The combined doping of the ceramics with Sb and other elements such as Li and Ta was shown to lead to the formation of orthorhombic-tetragonal phase boundaries in KNN-based ceramics at room temperature, which was found to improve the performance of KNN-based ceramics due to the effect of a polymorphic phase transition (PPT). Therefore, the temperature stability of KNN-based ceramics could be significantly improved, and they have since become a very attractive lead-free material for the fabrication of transducers, sensors, etc. To improve the sinterability of KNN-based ceramics, sintering aids such as CuO and MnO2 are often used along with Sb substitution. However, so far, little attention has been paid on the isolated effect of a substitution of Sb for Nb on the phase transition behavior of KNN ceramics, especially at temperatures close to room temperature , and the reason for the enhancement of the piezoelectricity reported for Sb-modified KNN. Recently, Sb-modified K0.52Na0.48NbO3 ceramics with 1mol% CuO added as sintering aid were found to exhibit an ultra-high electro-strain in a relatively low driving electric field range, which is promising for actuator applications. Hence, investigating the isolated effect of the substitution of Sb for Nb on the properties of pure KNN ceramics in more detail seems promising. Considering the effect of the Na/K content on the phase structure and the composition range for the occurrence of a morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) in (K, Na)NbO3 ceramics, we selected (K0.49Na0.51)NbO3 as target material and explored the effect of the substitution of Sb for Nb at the B-site on the crystal structure, microstructure, and piezoelectric and dielectric properties of KNN ceramics, especially focusing on the relationship between the structure and the macroscopic properties. It was found that the enhancement of the piezoelectric behavior of Sb-modified KNN ceramics is not caused by a PPT, but instead is closely related to an improvement of the microstructure induced by the substitution of Sb for Nb.

# CONCLUSION
In summary, the substitution of SbE^{5+} for NbE^{5+} at the B site of the KNN perovskite structure was found to increase the degree of structural symmetry. Adding an appropriate amount of Sb2O3 was found to promote the grain growth and enhance the degree of densification, thereby improving the piezoelectricity of the prepared KNN ceramics. The sample with x=0.06 showed a dense microstructure and the best ferroelectric properties with a piezoelectric constant d 33 of 182 pC/N, a planar electromechanical coupling factor k p of 41%, a remnant polarization P r of 24 \u03bcC/cmE^{2} and a coercive field E c of 1.06kV/mm.

# TITLE
The effect of B-site substitution on the structural evolution and electrical properties of lead-free (K,Na)NbO3 ceramics

# ABSTRACT
Lead-free (K0.49Na0.51)(Nb1−xSbx)O3 piezoelectric ceramics were prepared via the conventional sintering method and the effect of the substitution of Nb with Sb on the phase structure, microstructure and electrical properties of the prepared (K0.49Na0.51)(Nb1−xSbx)O3 ceramics was systematically investigated. The prepared ceramics exhibited a single-phase perovskite structure which changed from a standard orthorhombic structure to a pseudocubic structure as x was increased from 0 to 0.1. Substituting an appropriate amount of Sb5+ for Nb5+ was found to improve the microstructure and thereby enhance the piezoelectric/ferroelectric properties. The peak values of the piezoelectric constant d
                     33 (182 pC/N) and the electromechanical coupling coefficient k
                     p (41%) were obtained for the ceramic with x=0.06.

# INTRODUCTION
Lead-free (K0.5Na0.5)NbO3 (KNN)-based piezoceramics have attracted much attention since the breakthrough reported by Saito et al. and are regarded as one of the most promising candidates for replacing (Pb, Zr)TiO3 ceramics. However, the piezoelectric constant d 33 of KNN is pretty low. For KNN ceramics fabricated via conventional sintering, a d 33 of about 100 pC/N was reported, which could be increased to the highest currently reported value of around 160 pC/N when changing the fabrication technique to hot pressing. Researchers have made tremendous efforts to improve the properties of KNN-based ceramics through optimizing their composition. Recently, due to its high Pauling electronegativity, Sb has been used to improve the dielectric and piezoelectric properties of KNN-based ceramics. The combined doping of the ceramics with Sb and other elements such as Li and Ta was shown to lead to the formation of orthorhombic-tetragonal phase boundaries in KNN-based ceramics at room temperature, which was found to improve the performance of KNN-based ceramics due to the effect of a polymorphic phase transition (PPT). Therefore, the temperature stability of KNN-based ceramics could be significantly improved, and they have since become a very attractive lead-free material for the fabrication of transducers, sensors, etc. To improve the sinterability of KNN-based ceramics, sintering aids such as CuO and MnO2 are often used along with Sb substitution. However, so far, little attention has been paid on the isolated effect of a substitution of Sb for Nb on the phase transition behavior of KNN ceramics, especially at temperatures close to room temperature , and the reason for the enhancement of the piezoelectricity reported for Sb-modified KNN. Recently, Sb-modified K0.52Na0.48NbO3 ceramics with 1mol% CuO added as sintering aid were found to exhibit an ultra-high electro-strain in a relatively low driving electric field range, which is promising for actuator applications. Hence, investigating the isolated effect of the substitution of Sb for Nb on the properties of pure KNN ceramics in more detail seems promising. Considering the effect of the Na/K content on the phase structure and the composition range for the occurrence of a morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) in (K, Na)NbO3 ceramics, we selected (K0.49Na0.51)NbO3 as target material and explored the effect of the substitution of Sb for Nb at the B-site on the crystal structure, microstructure, and piezoelectric and dielectric properties of KNN ceramics, especially focusing on the relationship between the structure and the macroscopic properties. It was found that the enhancement of the piezoelectric behavior of Sb-modified KNN ceramics is not caused by a PPT, but instead is closely related to an improvement of the microstructure induced by the substitution of Sb for Nb.

# CONCLUSION
In summary, the substitution of SbE^{5+} for NbE^{5+} at the B site of the KNN perovskite structure was found to increase the degree of structural symmetry. Adding an appropriate amount of Sb2O3 was found to promote the grain growth and enhance the degree of densification, thereby improving the piezoelectricity of the prepared KNN ceramics. The sample with x=0.06 showed a dense microstructure and the best ferroelectric properties with a piezoelectric constant d 33 of 182 pC/N, a planar electromechanical coupling factor k p of 41%, a remnant polarization P r of 24 \u03bcC/cmE^{2} and a coercive field E c of 1.06kV/mm.
