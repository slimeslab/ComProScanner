# TITLE
Enhanced piezoelectric property and promoted depolarization temperature in Fe doped Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 lead-free ceramics

# ABSTRACT
However, a commonly accepted concept is that the promotion of depolarization temperature of Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based lead-free ceramics is usually companied by deterioration of piezoelectric properties. In the present study, the effects of acceptor-Fe doping on piezoelectric property and thermal depolarization behavior of Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 ceramics are investigated. Fe doping at an appropriate level (≤ 3.0%) improves piezoelectric property and thermal stability simultaneously, due to the stabilization of long-range ferroelectric order. Piezoelectric constant d
                     33 increases from 125 pC/N to 148 pC/N with Fe amount of 3.0%, and then decreases. The depolarization temperature T
                     d is promoted continuously with Fe addition, from 76°C for the undoped sample to 118°C for the sample with Fe amount of 5.0%. It is proposed that the piezoelectric property and thermal stability can be simultaneously improved by stabilizing the long-range ferroelectric order in Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based systems with obvious relaxor character. This work provides a new insight into the improvement of Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
For more than half a century, lead-based piezoelectrics, such as Pb(Zr, Ti)O3 (PZT), Pb(Mg1/3Nb2/3)O3-PbTiO3 (PMN-PT), etc., have been widely used in various types of piezoelectric devices, due to their excellent piezoelectric properties and good thermal stability. Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 (BNT)-based ceramics are expected to be one of the most promising lead-free candidates because of the superior piezoelectric property. The giant electric-field-induced strains discovered in BNT-based ceramics have further attracted considerable interests in the past few years. Unfortunately, the piezoelectric thermal stability of BNT-based ceramics is poor. Although they possess a high maximum dielectric constant temperature (T m ~ 300\xb0C), their depolarization temperature (T d), at which piezoelectric properties degrade, is usually far below T m. Thus, the low T d is the main drawback of BNT-based ceramics for these kinds of applications. It is of great importance to explore methods to promote depolarization temperature and further improve piezoelectric properties of BNT-based ceramics. There are currently two approaches to tailor the thermal depolarization behavior of BNT-based materials. It is reported recently that piezoelectric thermal stability is significantly enhanced by introducing ZnO to form BNT-based piezoceramic composites. For a long time, it is believed that there is an inverse relationship between d 33 and T d in BNT-based systems with substitutional modifications. For example, by adding tetragonal Bi1/2K1/2TiO3 (BKT) into the rhombohedral BNT, the piezoelectric properties are significantly improved due to the formation of morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) , whereas the depolarization temperature decreases rapidly down in the vicinity of MPB. As another example, Mn doping in 0.96BNT-0.04BiAlO3 was found to increase T d but decrease d 33. This inverse relationship between d 33 and T d used to be understood in terms of the stability of domain configuration , where higher T d means more stable domains, thus the contributions from domain wall motion are suppressed, consequently the d 33 decreases. However, recent studies call for a new insight into the relationship between d 33 and T d. Simultaneous reduction of both d 33 and T d was observed in BNT-BKT ceramics modified by (K,Na)NbO3 (KNN) , BiMeO3 (Me = Al, Sc, Mn) , and ZrE^{4+} , which implies that the inverse relationship between d 33 and T d cannot be a universal rule in BNT-based systems. The reduction of both d 33 and T d was found to be due to the disruption of long-range ferroelectric order. This founding reminds us the possibility of simultaneous improvement of d 33 and T d by stabilizing of ferroelectric order. In our previous work, a stabilization effect on the long-range ferroelectric order in Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 (BNKT, the MPB composition) by Fe doping was found. In this study, the effect of Fe doping on piezoelectric property and depolarization behavior of BNKT lead-free ceramics is investigated. The results show that Fe doping at an appropriate level improves both piezoelectric constant d 33 and depolarization temperature T d simultaneously.

# CONCLUSION
The electrical properties and thermal depolarization behavior of Fe doped Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 lead-free ceramics were investigated. Fe doping at an appropriate level (\u2264 3.0%) leads to the enhanced piezoelectric property and thermal stability, due to the stabilization of ferroelectric order. It is proposed that the piezoelectric properties and depolarization temperature can be simultaneously improved by stabilizing the long-range ferroelectric order in BNT-based systems with obvious relaxor character.

# TITLE
Enhanced piezoelectric property and promoted depolarization temperature in Fe doped Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 lead-free ceramics

# ABSTRACT
However, a commonly accepted concept is that the promotion of depolarization temperature of Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based lead-free ceramics is usually companied by deterioration of piezoelectric properties. In the present study, the effects of acceptor-Fe doping on piezoelectric property and thermal depolarization behavior of Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 ceramics are investigated. Fe doping at an appropriate level (≤ 3.0%) improves piezoelectric property and thermal stability simultaneously, due to the stabilization of long-range ferroelectric order. Piezoelectric constant d
                     33 increases from 125 pC/N to 148 pC/N with Fe amount of 3.0%, and then decreases. The depolarization temperature T
                     d is promoted continuously with Fe addition, from 76°C for the undoped sample to 118°C for the sample with Fe amount of 5.0%. It is proposed that the piezoelectric property and thermal stability can be simultaneously improved by stabilizing the long-range ferroelectric order in Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based systems with obvious relaxor character. This work provides a new insight into the improvement of Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3-based lead-free piezoelectric ceramics.

# INTRODUCTION
For more than half a century, lead-based piezoelectrics, such as Pb(Zr, Ti)O3 (PZT), Pb(Mg1/3Nb2/3)O3-PbTiO3 (PMN-PT), etc., have been widely used in various types of piezoelectric devices, due to their excellent piezoelectric properties and good thermal stability. Bi1/2Na1/2TiO3 (BNT)-based ceramics are expected to be one of the most promising lead-free candidates because of the superior piezoelectric property. The giant electric-field-induced strains discovered in BNT-based ceramics have further attracted considerable interests in the past few years. Unfortunately, the piezoelectric thermal stability of BNT-based ceramics is poor. Although they possess a high maximum dielectric constant temperature (T m ~ 300\xb0C), their depolarization temperature (T d), at which piezoelectric properties degrade, is usually far below T m. Thus, the low T d is the main drawback of BNT-based ceramics for these kinds of applications. It is of great importance to explore methods to promote depolarization temperature and further improve piezoelectric properties of BNT-based ceramics. There are currently two approaches to tailor the thermal depolarization behavior of BNT-based materials. It is reported recently that piezoelectric thermal stability is significantly enhanced by introducing ZnO to form BNT-based piezoceramic composites. For a long time, it is believed that there is an inverse relationship between d 33 and T d in BNT-based systems with substitutional modifications. For example, by adding tetragonal Bi1/2K1/2TiO3 (BKT) into the rhombohedral BNT, the piezoelectric properties are significantly improved due to the formation of morphotropic phase boundary (MPB) , whereas the depolarization temperature decreases rapidly down in the vicinity of MPB. As another example, Mn doping in 0.96BNT-0.04BiAlO3 was found to increase T d but decrease d 33. This inverse relationship between d 33 and T d used to be understood in terms of the stability of domain configuration , where higher T d means more stable domains, thus the contributions from domain wall motion are suppressed, consequently the d 33 decreases. However, recent studies call for a new insight into the relationship between d 33 and T d. Simultaneous reduction of both d 33 and T d was observed in BNT-BKT ceramics modified by (K,Na)NbO3 (KNN) , BiMeO3 (Me = Al, Sc, Mn) , and ZrE^{4+} , which implies that the inverse relationship between d 33 and T d cannot be a universal rule in BNT-based systems. The reduction of both d 33 and T d was found to be due to the disruption of long-range ferroelectric order. This founding reminds us the possibility of simultaneous improvement of d 33 and T d by stabilizing of ferroelectric order. In our previous work, a stabilization effect on the long-range ferroelectric order in Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 (BNKT, the MPB composition) by Fe doping was found. In this study, the effect of Fe doping on piezoelectric property and depolarization behavior of BNKT lead-free ceramics is investigated. The results show that Fe doping at an appropriate level improves both piezoelectric constant d 33 and depolarization temperature T d simultaneously.

# CONCLUSION
The electrical properties and thermal depolarization behavior of Fe doped Bi1/2(Na0.8K0.2)1/2TiO3 lead-free ceramics were investigated. Fe doping at an appropriate level (\u2264 3.0%) leads to the enhanced piezoelectric property and thermal stability, due to the stabilization of ferroelectric order. It is proposed that the piezoelectric properties and depolarization temperature can be simultaneously improved by stabilizing the long-range ferroelectric order in BNT-based systems with obvious relaxor character.
