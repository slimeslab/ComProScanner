# TITLE
Structural, dielectric, AC conductivity, piezoelectric and impedance spectroscopy studies on PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:RE3+(RE3+: La3+, Nd3+ and Dy3+) ceramics

# ABSTRACT
PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 with simultaneous addition of (RE3+:La3+, Nd3+, Dy3+) synthesized by conventional solid state reaction technique. The effect of La, Nd and Dy addition on structural, electrical and optical properties of PZT ceramic are investigated. Powder X-ray diffraction pattern shows that the pure perovskite structure and rare earth element completely diffused into the PZT lattice during sintering. Scanning electron microscope observation indicates that a small amount of La3+, Nd3+, Dy3+ ions does not affect the microstructure. Temperature and frequency dependent dielectric properties of rare earth doped PZT ceramic are investigated. The local micro structural heterogeneity has been induced to PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3: (RE3+: La3+, Nd3+, Dy3+) which is studied by Raman spectroscopy. The rare earth doped PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 ceramics have sensitive phase transition behavior from 275 to 310 °C. The impedance spectroscopic studies show that oxygen vacancies role in ionic conduction of rare earth ions doped PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 ceramics for different temperature. The PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:Nd3+ composition has formidable structural and electrical properties of low conductivity (~10–3 S/m) and low transition temperature (275 °C). At room temperature, this composition exhibits a high piezoelectric coefficient of (d33 = 149pC/N). The PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:RE3+ ceramics exhibits good thermal stability up to 400 °C that assure for high temperature piezoelectric applications.

# INTRODUCTION
After the first report by Buyanova et al. in 1965, the lead-based piezoelectric ceramics has been used for electromechanical and energy storage capacitor. High performance piezoelectricity has been observed in lanthanum, neodymium and multi-rare earth doped lead-based high temperature piezo ceramics that forms a new morphotropic phase boundary (MPB), which have excellent thermal stability.. In the demonstration of resonance and anti-resonance impedance modulus, at higher temperatures, the ceramics with 6 mol% LaE^{3+}/NdE^{3+} doping exhibits high piezoelectric coefficient and also the excited intrinsic oxygen vacancies are noted for electrical and dielectric relaxation processes. The SrE^{2+} addition in binary ceramics increased the diffuse phase transition at high temperature. GdE^{3+} modified PZT ceramics is showing fatigue-free behavior for long electrical cycles (~10E^{6} cycles). Doping in PbE^{2+} site by REE^{3+} element attributes significant character in modifying the electrical properties of the material and made the heterogeneity of lattice due to variation in oxygen coordination of (O-Ti-O-Zr (RE)-O) molecular arrangements. The increases in the covalent bonding nature and displacement of PbE^{2+} with addition of La in the imperfect perovskite region, causes structural heterogeneity variation in the lattice and thus improves the electromechanical properties of PZT ceramic. Perovskite structure based lead zirconate titanate PbZr1-xTixO3 ceramics are the most widely used ferroelectric and piezoelectric materials because of their excellent electrical and optical properties. Lead zirconate titanate is an inter metallic inorganic compound with the chemical formula Pb[ZrxTi1-x]O3. The main objective of this work is to prepare the PZT material doping with lanthanide and to study its dielectric parameters for various composition and temperatures at different KHz frequency. The additives used in compositional modification of PZT are the isovalent ones, i.e. PbE^{2+}, TiE^{4+} and ZrE^{4+} are replaced partially by other cations with the same chemical valence and ionic radii as those of the replaced ions. The rare-earth additives are donor type substitutions, known as soft dopants (LaE^{3+}, NdE^{3+}, DyE^{3+}, SbE^{3+}, BiE^{3+},NbE^{5+}, SbE^{5+}, WE^{6+} etc.. In general, REE^{3+} can occupy the A-sites to replace PbE^{2+} ions, extra positive charges are introduced into the lattice due to the fact that the valence of the doping ions is higher than that of PbE^{2+} ions, therefore, a lead vacancy is created in the lattice to maintain electro neutrality. When REE^{3+} ions enter into the perovskite lattice, they occupy the B-sites to replace ZrE^{4+} or TiE^{3+} ions associated with oxygen vacancies. In this article, the impact of rare earth ions on structural heterogeneity and dielectric dispersions of PZT system are analyzed for their enhanced electromechanical properties at high temperature.

The changes in the transition temperature for the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:DyE^{3+} and effective lowest resistance for the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:LaE^{3+} have been observed. The ceramics also exhibits good performance with piezoelectric constant d33 = 149pC/N. The ceramics exhibit good thermal stability, demonstrating that the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:REE^{3+} ceramics are useful candidates for high temperature piezoelectric applications.

Structural, dielectric, AC conductivity, piezoelectric and impedance spectroscopy studies on PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:RE3+(RE3+: La3+, Nd3+ and Dy3+) ceramics

# ABSTRACT
PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 with simultaneous addition of (RE3+:La3+, Nd3+, Dy3+) synthesized by conventional solid state reaction technique. # INTRODUCTION
After the first report by Buyanova et al. in 1965, the lead-based piezoelectric ceramics has been used for electromechanical and energy storage capacitor. High performance piezoelectricity has been observed in lanthanum, neodymium and multi-rare earth doped lead-based high temperature piezo ceramics that forms a new morphotropic phase boundary (MPB), which have excellent thermal stability.. In the demonstration of resonance and anti-resonance impedance modulus, at higher temperatures, the ceramics with 6 mol% LaE^{3+}/NdE^{3+} doping exhibits high piezoelectric coefficient and also the excited intrinsic oxygen vacancies are noted for electrical and dielectric relaxation processes. The SrE^{2+} addition in binary ceramics increased the diffuse phase transition at high temperature. GdE^{3+} modified PZT ceramics is showing fatigue-free behavior for long electrical cycles (~10E^{6} cycles). Doping in PbE^{2+} site by REE^{3+} element attributes significant character in modifying the electrical properties of the material and made the heterogeneity of lattice due to variation in oxygen coordination of (O-Ti-O-Zr (RE)-O) molecular arrangements. The increases in the covalent bonding nature and displacement of PbE^{2+} with addition of La in the imperfect perovskite region, causes structural heterogeneity variation in the lattice and thus improves the electromechanical properties of PZT ceramic. Perovskite structure based lead zirconate titanate PbZr1-xTixO3 ceramics are the most widely used ferroelectric and piezoelectric materials because of their excellent electrical and optical properties. Lead zirconate titanate is an inter metallic inorganic compound with the chemical formula Pb[ZrxTi1-x]O3. The main objective of this work is to prepare the PZT material doping with lanthanide and to study its dielectric parameters for various composition and temperatures at different KHz frequency. The additives used in compositional modification of PZT are the isovalent ones, i.e. PbE^{2+}, TiE^{4+} and ZrE^{4+} are replaced partially by other cations with the same chemical valence and ionic radii as those of the replaced ions. The rare-earth additives are donor type substitutions, known as soft dopants (LaE^{3+}, NdE^{3+}, DyE^{3+}, SbE^{3+}, BiE^{3+},NbE^{5+}, SbE^{5+}, WE^{6+} etc.. In general, REE^{3+} can occupy the A-sites to replace PbE^{2+} ions, extra positive charges are introduced into the lattice due to the fact that the valence of the doping ions is higher than that of PbE^{2+} ions, therefore, a lead vacancy is created in the lattice to maintain electro neutrality. When REE^{3+} ions enter into the perovskite lattice, they occupy the B-sites to replace ZrE^{4+} or TiE^{3+} ions associated with oxygen vacancies. In this article, the impact of rare earth ions on structural heterogeneity and dielectric dispersions of PZT system are analyzed for their enhanced electromechanical properties at high temperature.

# CONCLUSION
In this article, the effect of rare earth (REE^{3+}:LaE^{3+}, NdE^{3+}, DyE^{3+}) doped PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 ceramics was synthesized using conventional solid state reaction technique. Effects of La, Nd and Dy in structural, electrical and optical properties of PZT ceramic were studied. The local micro structural heterogeneity induced to PZT by LaE^{3+}, NdE^{3+}, DyE^{3+} are studied by Raman spectroscopy. LaE^{3+}, NdE^{3+}, DyE^{3+} doped PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3 ceramics have sensitive phase transition behavior from 275 to 310 \xb0C. The impedance spectroscopic studies show that oxygen vacancies role in ionic conduction of PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:REE^{3+} ceramics for different temperature. The PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:NdE^{3+} material has formidable structural and electrical properties at low conductivity (~10E^{\u20133} S/m) and low transition temperature (275 \xb0C). A diffused dielectric anomalies were observed between 300 and 400 \xb0C attributed to the dielectric relaxation process. The changes in the transition temperature for the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:DyE^{3+} and effective lowest resistance for the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:LaE^{3+} have been observed. The ceramics also exhibits good performance with piezoelectric constant d33 = 149pC/N. The ceramics exhibit good thermal stability, demonstrating that the PbZr0.52Ti0.48O3:REE^{3+} ceramics are useful candidates for high temperature piezoelectric applications.
