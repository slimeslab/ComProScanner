# INTRODUCTION
Multiferroic BiFeO3 shows ferroelectric (FE) order below Curie temperature of T C\u2212FE=830\xb0 C and G-type anti-ferromagnetic (AFM) order below Neel temperature of T N=370\xb0 C. In addition to the ferroelectric, magnetic and magnetoelectric properties, its piezoelectric property has attracted a lot of attention recently benefiting from the Pb-free and high T C\u2212FE traits. Especially, an enhanced piezoelectric d 33 of \u223c110 pm/V coefficient was reported in strained BiFeO3 epitaxial thin film and Sm-doped BiFeO3 epitaxial film. With partial replacement of BiE^{3+} ion (radius=1.030 \xc5) by SmE^{3+} ion (radius=0.958 \xc5), Bi0.84Sm0.16FeO3 epitaxial film could be reversibly converted between the rhombohedral phase and the orthorhombic phase, which allows an enhanced d 33 of \u223c100 pm/V under an applied electric field of 500 kV/cm. Synchrotron X-ray diffraction reveals substantial coexistence of ferroelectric rhombohedral R3c phase and anti-ferroelectric (AFE) PbZrO3-like orthorhombic Pbam phase in Sm doped BiFeO3 epitaxial film. We argued that Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics may coexist with a rhombohedral-like phase and orthorhombic-like phase at a certain x, which should permit an enhanced d 33 value. In spite of intensive research of Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 films, there are few reports concerning the piezoelectric, T C\u2212FE and their dependences on Sm concentration in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics. Pb-free Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics may show some different characteristics than Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 epitaxial film since films could be influenced by substrate, strain, and composition fluctuation to some extent. Although we know that BiFeO3 has the T C\u2212FE of 813 \xb0C, it is necessary to know the dependence of ferroelectric T C\u2212FE on Sm concentration. The low-temperature ferroelectric phase may transform to high-temperature orthorhombic phase in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 at a certain x. In this letter, we study the dependencies of crystal structure, ferroelectric polarization, piezoelectric d 33 value, T C\u2212FE and magnetic properties on Sm concentration in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 (x=0\u20130.25) ceramics.

# CONCLUSION
The room-temperature Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 shows a ferroelectric rhombohedral-like phase at x=0\u20130.12, coexists with the ferroelectric triclinic phase and anti-ferroelectric orthorhombic phase at x=0.125\u20130.2, and transforms to the paraelectric orthoferrite type phase at x=0.25. This coexistence enhances the piezoelectric d 33 value to 42 pC/N at x=0.125. Besides, Bi0.875Sm0.125FeO3 shows piezoelectric traits at high temperature such as 700 \xb0C that should come from ferroelectric clusters inside a non-ferroelectric orthorhombic phase.

# INTRODUCTION
Multiferroic BiFeO3 shows ferroelectric (FE) order below Curie temperature of T C\u2212FE=830\xb0 C and G-type anti-ferromagnetic (AFM) order below Neel temperature of T N=370\xb0 C. In addition to the ferroelectric, magnetic and magnetoelectric properties, its piezoelectric property has attracted a lot of attention recently benefiting from the Pb-free and high T C\u2212FE traits. Especially, an enhanced piezoelectric d 33 of \u223c110 pm/V coefficient was reported in strained BiFeO3 epitaxial thin film and Sm-doped BiFeO3 epitaxial film. With partial replacement of BiE^{3+} ion (radius=1.030 \xc5) by SmE^{3+} ion (radius=0.958 \xc5), Bi0.84Sm0.16FeO3 epitaxial film could be reversibly converted between the rhombohedral phase and the orthorhombic phase, which allows an enhanced d 33 of \u223c100 pm/V under an applied electric field of 500 kV/cm. Synchrotron X-ray diffraction reveals substantial coexistence of ferroelectric rhombohedral R3c phase and anti-ferroelectric (AFE) PbZrO3-like orthorhombic Pbam phase in Sm doped BiFeO3 epitaxial film. We argued that Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics may coexist with a rhombohedral-like phase and orthorhombic-like phase at a certain x, which should permit an enhanced d 33 value. In spite of intensive research of Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 films, there are few reports concerning the piezoelectric, T C\u2212FE and their dependences on Sm concentration in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics. Pb-free Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 ceramics may show some different characteristics than Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 epitaxial film since films could be influenced by substrate, strain, and composition fluctuation to some extent. Although we know that BiFeO3 has the T C\u2212FE of 813 \xb0C, it is necessary to know the dependence of ferroelectric T C\u2212FE on Sm concentration. The low-temperature ferroelectric phase may transform to high-temperature orthorhombic phase in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 at a certain x. In this letter, we study the dependencies of crystal structure, ferroelectric polarization, piezoelectric d 33 value, T C\u2212FE and magnetic properties on Sm concentration in Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 (x=0\u20130.25) ceramics.

# CONCLUSION
The room-temperature Bi1\u2212x Sm x FeO3 shows a ferroelectric rhombohedral-like phase at x=0\u20130.12, coexists with the ferroelectric triclinic phase and anti-ferroelectric orthorhombic phase at x=0.125\u20130.2, and transforms to the paraelectric orthoferrite type phase at x=0.25. This coexistence enhances the piezoelectric d 33 value to 42 pC/N at x=0.125. Besides, Bi0.875Sm0.125FeO3 shows piezoelectric traits at high temperature such as 700 \xb0C that should come from ferroelectric clusters inside a non-ferroelectric orthorhombic phase.
